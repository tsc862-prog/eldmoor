# Campaign seed: The Wendmouth (150x68)

DM-only. The heroes never read this; reveal it through narrate, npc_say, hooks, staged scenes and marks. This is the second chapter, levels 5 to 10: a company walks in from the Hollin Reach up the Drovers' Road, around level 6, with a Reach's worth of small troubles behind it. Two towns a long day apart, one bridge, one sea. Keep it wider than the Reach, not busier.

## Premise
- **Where The Road Comes Out**: The Drovers' Road ends on the south bank of the Wend. For a company out of Oldstead this is the first country that does not know their names. Nobody here has heard of the Hollin Reach except as the place the stock comes from. tags: premise, company
- **One Bridge**: The Low Bridge is the only way over the Wend on this map, and it stands under Gullstrand's quay. Everything between the harbour and the fen pays for that: in time, in tolls, in who gets to say what the river is doing. tags: premise, bridge
- **A River That Runs Downhill**: Whatever goes wrong upriver reaches the mouth last, and the mouth is where people notice. Fishers at Gullstrand say the Wend runs a hand's breadth off its old line each morning. Nobody has walked the Wend Road to ask why. tags: premise, river

## Threads
- **The Wandering Bed**: Mark a drowned post on the bank that was dry last week. Each week the line moves further, always toward the south bank first. Stiltmere's people have started counting their stilts. tags: river, act1
- **The Smugglers' Lamp**: Lights move in the Sallowfen reeds at night, and the Sallow Vaults are said to be open again. A salt-run that never reaches Stiltmere is the first anyone hears of it. tags: sallow, vaults, act1
- **The Nets**: Something has been at the nets and the drying racks on the Gullhead shore, and the gulls will not nest near it. The net-menders would pay, and they would rather not say how much it has already cost. tags: den, gullhead, act1
- **The Long Road Between**: A carter who left Gullstrand for Stiltmere did not arrive, and his mule did. It is most of a day's walk, the flats start at the Vaults' turning, and the road is nobody's to keep. tags: road, sedge
- **Old Mother Sedge**: The matriarch of the fen is hunting nearer the road than she used to. Every lurker in the flats is one of hers. tags: sedge, elite
- **The Wend Road**: Past Stiltmere the road runs on upriver toward the old weir. It stops at the map's edge for now: an order to walk it is refused in words. Keep hinting at what lies up it, and never promise an arrival. tags: roads, act2

## Characters
- **Gate Captain of Gullstrand**: Harbour post, salt-hoarse, tired of fish politics. Wants proof the river is moving before the harbour dues are cut. Secret: he has been moving the harbour posts himself each morning to hide how far the water has gone. tags: gullstrand, npc
- **Stiltmere's Reedmaster**: Fen town, speaks softly and counts his stilts. Wants the Sallow smugglers stopped, quietly. Secret: his own cousin runs the salt. tags: stiltmere, npc
- **Captain Dane Sallow**: Smuggler-captain of the Sallow Vaults. Charming, a little vain, counts every barrel. Wants the road open and the receipts selling. tags: dane, vaults
- **Old Brine**: A scarred sea-wolf mother denned under the roots of the Gull Cliffs, fat on the net-menders' catch. An animal, hungry and guarding young, not a villain. tags: den, gullhead
- **Old Mother Sedge**: The worst thing on this map, and she does not know the map has an edge. Keep her off the road until the company has seen what her young can do. tags: sedge, elite

## Plans
- **Act I, levels 5-7, Wendmouth**: The arrival, the crossways and the choice it makes them take, the Low Bridge, Gullstrand's board. Teach the width: the two towns do not know each other's news. tags: act1
- **Act II, levels 7-10, Sallowfen and Gullhead**: The long road east, Stiltmere, the Vaults, the den. Let the company choose which town it answers to; sides here are old stories, not a border. tags: act2
- **If they came from the Reach**: Whatever they carried up the road is the only thing here that knows them: a milestone's number, a name out of a ledger, Oldstead's own word. Let one thing from the Reach turn out to have been heard of here, and once. tags: roads, hollin
- **The Way Back Stays Open**: The Drovers' Road runs both ways. A company may go home; the Reach will be as they left it, and they will have outgrown it. tags: roads, hollin
- **If the company falls**: Death is permanent. A replacement walks in at the next camp or town; a stranger from these parts, who has never heard of the Reach. tags: hardcore

## Secrets
- **The Harbour Posts**: The gate captain moves his marker posts each night. The river's true line, taken from the drowned post on the bank, shows how far it has gone. Where: a night watch at the quay, or his own confession. tags: gullstrand, river, reveal
- **The Forged Receipts**: Captain Dane Sallow's forgeries pass better than honest coin, and they are written for a toll on a ford further up the Wend. Where: The Salt Tally. tags: dane, forgery, reveal
- **The Same Count**: The tally-marks in the Counting Room are the ones Foreman Ruddock chalks on his slate in the Reach. Someone keeps both books. Where: The Counting Room, set beside anything brought from the Quarry Cellars. tags: vaults, hollin, reveal
