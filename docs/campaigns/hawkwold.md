# Campaign seed: The Hawkwold (150x66)

DM-only. The heroes never read this; reveal it through narrate, npc_say, hooks, staged scenes and marks. This is the third chapter, levels 6 to 11: a company walks in from the Hollin Reach up the Fell Road, around level 6 or 7, onto open upland a long way from anywhere it has been. Two towns across a stream, one ford of road between them, a barrow and a cairn. Keep it wide and windy, not busy.

## Premise
- **Where The Fell Road Comes Out**: The Fell Road climbs out of Crowfell and comes down onto sheepwalks nobody in Oldstead has ever described. The first stranger a company meets here has not heard of the Hollin Reach, and does not much care. tags: premise, company
- **Two Banks, One Bridge**: The Wend is a stream here, and the Wend Bridge is the only way over it on this map. Hawkstead keeps the north bank and Hornridge the south, and each has an opinion of the other's sheep. Sides here are old stories, not a border. tags: premise, bridge
- **High Ground, Long Sight**: Everything on the wold can be seen from a long way off, and so can you. The falconers say the hawks have started leaving the ridge-tops early. Nobody has asked what the hawks saw. tags: premise, hawks

## Threads
- **The Counted Flock**: The old herder in the cairn above the sheepwalks is up and counting sheep that are not there, and something small has been keeping the lambs from the lower fold at night. The shepherds would pay, and would rather not say how many they have lost. tags: cairn, foldlands, act1
- **The Kestrel Barrow**: A falconer-lord is buried in a grassed mound in the hills east of Hawkstead, with his birds. The lintel is cut with kestrels. Lately the mound is warm to stand on in the morning, and the tarrows have stopped grazing there. tags: barrow, hawkwold, act1
- **Greymane**: A grey wolf grown huge on the uplands' flocks hunts the wold between the road and the ridge. Shepherds call him the Pack-Father and set their dogs on a chain at night. tags: greymane, elite
- **The Long Slope**: Hornridge stands a long walk from the bridge, on the far side of the stream, and the south bank's road is nobody's to keep. A horn-trader left Hornridge for Hawkstead and has not been seen; his cart came back, with the horn still in it. tags: road, hornridge, act1
- **The Hawk Road**: Past the Barrow's turning the road runs on east to the map's edge. It is not open yet: an order to walk it is refused in words. Keep hinting that travellers set out on it and turn back, and never promise an arrival. tags: roads, act2

## Characters
- **Gate Captain of Hawkstead**: Tarrow, weathered, long-memoried and slow to speak. Wants the Barrow quiet before the autumn fair. Secret: she has already sent a patrol down it and it did not come back; the hall does not know. tags: hawkstead, npc
- **Hornridge's Horn-Warden**: Thornhide, heavy-browed, counts every tusk that comes into the hold. Wants the road to Hawkstead kept open and the horn-trade honest. Secret: the missing trader owed the hold a great deal. tags: hornridge, npc
- **Shepherd Wick**: A herder buried on the ridge sixty years ago, who will not be told the last flock is gone. A small sad haunting, with his dogs' bones about him; not a villain. tags: cairn, wick
- **Lord Merrin Hawkwright**: The falconer-lord of the Barrow. He bred the birds that bred the town's name, and sits among his perches in the Mews. Proud and entirely dead. tags: barrow, merrin
- **Greymane**: The map's one elite, a wolf the size of a pony. Keep him off the road until the company has seen what the flock-wolves can do. tags: greymane, elite

## Plans
- **Act I, levels 6-8, Foldlands and Hawkstead**: The arrival, the crossroads of the Fell Road, the cairn, the climb to Hawkstead and its terraces. Teach the width and the wind: the two towns do not know each other's news. tags: act1
- **Act II, levels 8-11, Hawkwold and Hornmoor**: The Barrow in the hills, the Wend Bridge, the long south bank, Hornridge. Let the company choose which hall it answers to. tags: act2
- **If they came from the Reach**: Whatever they carried up the road is the only thing here that knows them: a milestone's number, a name out of a ledger, Oldstead's own word. Let one thing from the Reach turn out to have been heard of here, and once. tags: roads, hollin
- **The Way Back Stays Open**: The Fell Road runs both ways. A company may go home; the Reach will be as they left it, and they will have outgrown it. tags: roads, hollin
- **If the company falls**: Death is permanent. A replacement walks in at the next camp or town; a stranger from these parts, who has never heard of the Reach. tags: hardcore

## Secrets
- **The Patrol That Did Not Return**: The captain's patrol went down the Kestrel Barrow a week ago and is still there, in the Niches, among the heaps of bone. Where: the Kestrel Niches, or her own confession over a cup. tags: hawkstead, barrow, reveal
- **The Trader's Debt**: The horn-trader never left the hold; he is held in a loft over the tusk-store until his debt is paid, and his cart was sent out to look like a loss. Where: the Horn-Warden's ledger, or the tusk-store loft. tags: hornridge, road, reveal
- **The Lure-Glove**: The glove the dead in the Niches hold is the lord's own, and it carries his mark. Set beside any hawk-feather from the ridge it is the same bird's. Someone still flies one. Where: the Kestrel Niches, set beside anything found on the wold. tags: barrow, hawks, reveal
