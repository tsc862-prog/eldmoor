# Campaign seed: The Wend's Head (160x76)

DM-only. The heroes never read this; reveal it through narrate, npc_say, hooks, staged scenes and marks. This is the fifth and last chapter, levels 14 to 22 (heroes stop at 20): a company comes in by the Kilnway from the Weirgate at about level 14, and the road ends here. One town, one great kiln, a cold crown, a spring pool and a wyrm. Keep it wide and quiet, and let the walk east feel like leaving every map behind.

## Premise
- **The End of the Road**: The Kilnway has one town on it, and past the town it has a kiln, a post and a pool. There is no road onward and nobody here talks as if there were. A company that has walked this far is asked, kindly, what it expects to find. tags: premise, company, roads
- **The River Begins Here**: The Wend does not come from the hills; it rises from a round pool under the rock at the crown's edge, and the pool is lower than the old marks say. Every mill on the river below, to the sea, is paying for it. tags: premise, spring, river
- **Heat Against the Spring**: Kragmaw sleeps in a ring of lava on the far side of the pool, and its heat is drying the spring. Whoever mends the weir, frees the warden or lets the river fail has to decide what to do about the wyrm first, and the Kilnmark folk have been deciding for a generation. tags: premise, kragmaw

## Threads
- **The Kiln Still Burns**: The Brandwright Kiln has been red at the heart since before the town, tended by a clay warden that was never told it could stop. Green stone for the weir below is fired only here. Kilnmark's smiths want the fire out and the stone made; its priests want the warden left alone. tags: kiln, dungeon, act1
- **Grimbrand**: The last kiln-tender's guardian, gone wild among the brickyards, walks its old rounds with a bellows for one arm. Carters on the Kilnway say they hear it wheeze before they see it. Keep it in the Kilnlands, and let the company see what the land's creatures can do first. tags: grimbrand, elite
- **Coldwatch**: A post at the edge of the crown, three men and a lamp, that keeps a count of who goes by to the pool and who comes back. It is a short count. They will say what they have seen of the wyrm's smoke, and never what they think of the people who go. tags: coldwatch, kragmaw, act2
- **The Wyrm Does Not Wake**: Kragmaw has not come out of its ring in living memory; raiders go to it. Hearth-stories say it was a hearth-keeper once. Nobody here knows if it can be killed for good, only that the lava ring cools when it falls, and that it wakes again. tags: kragmaw, raid, act2
- **Last Roof**: Kilnmark is the end of the Concord's freight and the last place a hero who falls can be buried in sight of a hall. The gate-folk keep a book of the names carried up the Kilnway. tags: kilnmark, act1

## Characters
- **Gate Captain of Kilnmark**: Tarrow, taciturn and long-memoried. Wants the Kilnway kept open to the freight and the pool watched. Secret: the book of names carried up the Kilnway has one name she has crossed out and written back four times. tags: kilnmark, npc
- **The Kiln-Warden**: A golem built to tend the fire. Not a villain; a thing that has never been relieved, and a mirror of Orsk at the weir below. DM-only: it will stop if somebody tells it, in the kiln-tenders' words. tags: kiln, warden
- **Coldwatch's Lamp-Keeper**: A weathered man with a lamp he never lets go out, who knows how the pool has fallen by the marks on the rock. He is unafraid of the wyrm and afraid of the snow. tags: coldwatch, npc
- **Kragmaw**: The Cinder Wyrm. Not a speaker. Keep it a presence: heat, a sound from beyond the pool, the lava ring's colour at night. tags: kragmaw
- **Grimbrand**: The map's one elite. A golem gone wild, still keeping its rounds. tags: grimbrand, elite

## Plans
- **Act I, levels 14-17, the Slagmoor and Kilnmark**: The arrival by the Kilnway, the town, the gate-book and the first sight of the kilns on the horizon. Teach the width and the quiet. tags: act1
- **Act II, levels 16-20, the Kilnlands**: The Brandwright Kiln, Grimbrand, and the brick-red country between; let the company choose whether to put the fire out. tags: act2
- **Act III, levels 18-20, the Crownfell**: Coldwatch, the pool, the lair. A raid is the company's own start; the DM may hint, never ask. The pool is the answer to the Vale's question. tags: act3, kragmaw
- **The Way Back Stays Open**: The Kilnway runs both ways. A company may go home to the Weirgate; the wyrm sleeps on the Wend's Head and nowhere else. tags: roads
- **If the company falls**: Death is permanent. A replacement walks in at the next camp or town; a stranger from these parts. tags: hardcore

## Secrets
- **The Crossed-Out Name**: The name the gate captain keeps writing back is Orsk's: the weir-keeper's oath was sworn at the pool, and the Kilnmark folk sent the first keepers down the Wend with its first stone. Where: the gate-book, or her own confession over a cup. tags: kilnmark, weir, reveal
- **The Falling Pool**: The marks cut on the rock at the pool's edge are the weir-keepers' tally of its height, in the same script as the Green Keeper's receipts, and the last twelve are each lower than the one before. Where: Coldwatch's lamp-keeper, or the rock itself. tags: spring, reveal
- **What Cools the Ring**: The lava ring cools to black rock when the wyrm falls, and the spring pool rises a hand's breadth by the next dawn, to fall again as it wakes. Where: Coldwatch's marks after a raid, or a hero who watches the pool the morning after. tags: kragmaw, spring, reveal
