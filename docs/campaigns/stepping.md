# Campaign seed: The Stepping Lands (160x100)

DM-only. The heroes never read this; reveal it through narrate, npc_say, hooks, staged scenes and marks. This is a test continent, so the story is about distance: a long stair of towns and fires, and who climbs it. Everything starts on the Fellowship's road. Townsfolk names are rolled; roles are canon.

## Premise
- **The Pilgrim's Stair**: Four home towns, six regions and one old road of waymark-cairns run from the western meadow to the pale tundra. The cairns were stepping stones for pilgrims walking to Palereach, and they are going dark in order, from the far end back. Something at the top is calling the pilgrims home, or calling them in. tags: premise, stair, pilgrims
- **Four Towns, One Debt**: Highgate and Cairnmoot of the Concord, Grimhold and Murkstead of the Warband each keep one piece of the old pilgrim-compact and do not know the others hold theirs. Together they say what Palereach is owed, and by whom. tags: premise, compact, towns

## Threads
- **The Dark Waymarks**: Waymark cairns on the roads out of Lowmark and Sedgewater hold a stub of candle at their base. Lately the stubs are wet, and the cairns face the wrong way. Mark a cairn near the Fellowship; let one be found moved overnight. tags: waymark, lowmark, act1
- **Pilgrims With No Gate**: Lost pilgrims on every road, all walking the same way and all saying their cairn-keeper sent them. None remember a keeper's name. Use the pilgrim encounter and a single arrival at a town gate. tags: pilgrims, roads
- **Hollowfang's Season**: The Old Wolf of Lowmark follows the pilgrim roads and guards one traveller at a time. Whoever he guards later vanishes. A short elite thread early on. tags: hollowfang, elite, act1
- **The Door at the Hollow Cairn**: In northern Cairnwold the Hollow Cairn shows only its mouth; the door has not yet opened. Treat it as sealed: marks, rumours, a Cairn-Door Key. Do not stage a dungeon run there. tags: hollow-cairn, key, act2
- **Murkstead Salt, Cairnmoot Wool**: The two river-towns trade across the Greywater under a truce neither faction admits. A caravan gone missing on the Sedgewater ford tests it. tags: trade, greywater, act2
- **The Ram-King's Toll**: Brask Ironhorn in Cairnwold holds the high pass to the Cinderreach road; hunters say he does not eat what he kills, he arranges it. tags: brask, cairnwold, act2

## Characters
- **Gate Captain of Highgate**: Concord gate post, far north-west. Writes the first pilgrim letter to Cairnmoot and wonders why no reply returns. Dry, careful. Secret: her own mother walked the Stair and was not seen again. tags: highgate, npc
- **Gate Captain of Cairnmoot**: The cairn-ringed hill market on the Greywater; Tarrow, taciturn, long-memoried. Wants the cairns relit before the herders ask. Secret: he knows the compact's second page and has told no one. tags: cairnmoot, tarrow, npc
- **Gate Warden of Grimhold**: Warband gate post, far south-west. Pits-raised, blunt. Wants the Fellowship back by dusk. Secret: the Ashfolk have a salt-song for the Stair that stops at the same verse every time. tags: grimhold, ashfolk, npc
- **Gate Warden of Murkstead**: Mirekin salt-boiler's town on stilts. Patient, poisonous, reads weather in steam. Secret: trades with Cairnmoot on the quiet and has done for years. tags: murkstead, mirekin
- **Hesper Wain, Cairn-Keeper**: The last of the waymark keepers, walking a long circuit with a lantern and a leather ledger. Wants help to relight the cairns in order. Voice: kind, never finishes a sentence about herself. Secret: the order is the point; the last cairn is Palereach. tags: keeper, waymark, ally
- **Brask Ironhorn**: The Ram-King of the Cairnwold, one horn snapped, the other grown into his shoulder. Wants the pass left alone. Voice: none; he speaks through arrangements of bone and stone. tags: brask, elite
- **Sister Maud Orrin, Pilgrim-Priest**: A Concord priest walking the Stair against her bishop's wishes, with a pack of tithe-seals. Wants a Fellowship to guard her to Rimeholt. Secret: her tithe-seals have no box; she is delivering the Chapel Tithe-Seal to a door that is not there yet. tags: priest, pilgrim, rimeholt

## Plans
- **Act I, levels 1-5, Lowmark**: Wolves at the gate and the first dark waymark. Teach the Fellowship the Stair: cairn, candle-stub, pilgrim. Meet Hesper Wain at a camp mark. Keep it small: two towns' worth of road, one elite rumour (Hollowfang). Ends on a cairn that goes out in their hands. tags: act1
- **Act II, levels 6-12, Sedgewater, Cairnwold, Crypt**: The truce-trade caravan on the Sedgewater ford; the Sunken Crypt in the south of Sedgewater (Vesk's drowned court holds the second cairn-key); Brask's pass in Cairnwold; the Hollow Cairn shown, never entered. Both factions discover the compact has four pieces. tags: act2
- **Act III, levels 13-20, Cinderreach, Rimeholt, Palereach**: Kragmaw burns on the west edge of Cinderreach; whoever tears out the Molten Eye learns it lit the first beacon. Rimeholt's Whitemaw tests the Fellowship at the edge of sight. Palereach holds Ulfryn Coldmarrow and the last cairn. Choose: relight the Stair, leave it dark, or walk to the end. tags: act3
- **Opening beats, days 1-4**: Day 1: the gate's wolves writ and a moved cairn on the Lowmark road. Day 2: a lost pilgrim at the gate, asks for Hesper by name. Day 3: Hesper at a camp mark with a lantern and a ledger. Day 4: Hollowfang rumoured; a Cairn-Door Key token in someone's pack. If a member falls, their oath passes to the replacement and Hesper keeps a page for them. tags: openings
- **Concord Fellowship (Highgate or Cairnmoot)**: Highgate starts farthest and has the letter that was never answered; Cairnmoot starts on the Greywater with the ford and the missing caravan. Speak in formal, dry terms. tags: concord, adapt
- **Warband Fellowship (Grimhold or Murkstead)**: Grimhold hears the salt-song's missing verse; Murkstead hears about the quiet trade with Cairnmoot. Speak bluntly. Neither knows Hesper; she finds them. tags: warband, adapt

## Secrets
- **The Cairns Are a Calendar**: They were set so each lit cairn marks one stage of a pilgrim's walk; dark cairns mean pilgrims are still walking and not arriving. Where: Hesper's ledger (a Lore roll), or a mystery mark at a Lowmark waymark. tags: cairns, reveal
- **The Compact Has Four Pages**: One page in each home town. Highgate: debts; Cairnmoot: the route; Grimhold: the salt-song; Murkstead: the names of those who never returned. Where: a gate captain's secret, revealed by a staged social scene. tags: compact, towns
- **Ulfryn Is the First Pilgrim**: The Tarn-King of Palereach was the first to walk the Stair and the last to light its end. The wights are his company. Where: a Lore roll at the last cairn, or Sister Maud's tithe-seal. tags: ulfryn, palereach
- **The Hollow Cairn Waits for the Whole Compact**: The door opens only when all four pages are read aloud at its mouth. Where: a staged mystery scene with the Cairn-Door Key; do not open any dungeon run. tags: hollow-cairn, key
