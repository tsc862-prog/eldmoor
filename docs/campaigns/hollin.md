# Campaign seed: The Hollin Reach (118x64)

DM-only. The heroes never read this; reveal it through narrate, npc_say, hooks, staged scenes and marks. This is a first chapter, not a world: one small walled town, a long road, a river nobody crosses. Keep it small and human. Everything starts on the company's road.

## Premise
- **The Near End Of The Road**: Oldstead is where the carts turn round. The Hollin Reach is the last country anybody here can name, and the two roads out run on past the last milestone. Nobody in town has walked them, and nobody will say so aloud. tags: premise, oldstead
- **A Town That Has Stopped Sending**: The tithe carts used to go out to the grange and come back. They stopped coming back, and Oldstead has quietly stopped sending them. The hall would like somebody else to find out why. tags: premise, grange
- **What The Company Is**: Strangers given a bed in the one hall on the map, on the understanding that they are worth feeding. Nobody here has a war to fight, only a wood that is closer than it used to be. tags: premise, company

## Threads
- **The Tithe Road**: The wood road ends at the Tithe Grange, and the ledgers there record carts that nobody in Oldstead remembers sending. Let a name in the ledger be one the townsfolk know. tags: grange, act1
- **Old Crookjaw's Season**: The lame old wolf of Crowfell has started coming down the Fell Road after dark, one milestone lower each week. Shepherds have stopped going up. tags: crookjaw, crowfell, elite
- **The Last Milestone**: Each road ends in a stone with a number cut in it. The two stones do not agree with each other, or with the town's own count. Somebody cut them a long time ago for a reason. tags: roads
- **Withy Water**: The Withy has no bridge and no ford, and the town has a rule about it older than the hall: nobody swims, and nobody lights a fire on the far bank. Nobody has tried in living memory, and lately somebody over there has been lighting one. tags: river

- **The Quarry Slate**: The Quarry Cellars under Crowfell shut before anyone in Oldstead was born, yet the slate on the stair-rail is chalked fresh. Once the first delve is done, let a shepherd or the gate captain mention it. tags: quarry, act2

## Characters
- **Gate Captain of Oldstead**: Keeps the east gate and the roads book. Dry, tired, fair. Wants a company she can send past the crossways. Secret: she has walked the Drovers' Road as far as the last milestone, and turned back, and never said why. tags: oldstead, npc
- **Cellarer Thrupp**: Kept the tithe for a priory nobody remembers. Stubborn, hungry, counts everything. Wants it in writing. tags: grange, delve
- **Foreman Ruddock**: Keeps the Quarry Cellars and the shift's names on a slate, and takes any company for the shift that is late. Voice: flat, exact, unsurprised. Wants the count to come out right. tags: quarry, delve
- **Old Crookjaw**: A very old wolf with a broken jaw healed wrong. An animal, not a villain, and the worst thing on this map. tags: crookjaw, elite

## Plans
- **Act I, levels 1-3, Hollinlea**: The first delve, the walk home, the gate captain's first jobs. Teach the walk: everything here is a long way off. tags: act1
- **Act II, levels 3-5, Withywood and Crowfell**: Hunting further out, Old Crookjaw, the milestones, the Quarry Cellars. Let the company grow past the town. tags: act2
- **The Roads Out**: The Drovers' Road is open: a company that orders it stands at the last milestone and decides, all together, at the table; it comes out on the Wendmouth, a level 5 to 10 land, so let a level 6 company feel the Reach has nothing left for it. The Fell Road is not open: a walk ordered along it is refused in words. Keep hinting at what lies past it, and never promise an arrival. tags: roads
- **Opening beats, days 1-4**: Day 1: the first delve. Day 2: a name in the ledger. Day 3: a shepherd down from Crowfell with a torn coat. Day 4: a light on the far bank of the Withy. tags: act1
- **If the company falls**: Death is permanent. A replacement walks in at the next camp or town; let the town notice the empty bed. tags: hardcore

## Prologue
- **The first delve**: The company's first dungeon is the Tithe Grange at the end of the wood road: read `first_delve` in dm_status. It is a long walk from the town, and the road goes no further. Keep it small: thieves in a cellar, an old man and his ledgers. One line in a ledger should point back at Oldstead. tags: prologue

## Secrets
- **The Same Hand**: The names on Ruddock's slate are chalked in the hand that kept Thrupp's later ledgers. Where: The Foreman's Tally, set beside a ledger from The Cellarer's Count. tags: quarry, grange, reveal
- **The Ledgers Are Current**: Thrupp's ledgers run on past the year the priory fell, and the entries are in more than one hand. Someone still comes to collect. Where: The Cellarer's Count. tags: grange, reveal
- **The Far Bank**: Whoever lights the fire on the far bank of the Withy is waiting for someone from Oldstead, by arrangement, and has been for years. Where: the gate captain, or the roads book. tags: river, reveal
