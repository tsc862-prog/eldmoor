# Campaign seed: The Weirgate (150x68)

DM-only. The heroes never read this; reveal it through narrate, npc_say, hooks, staged scenes and marks. This is the fourth chapter, levels 10 to 15: a company comes in along the Wend Road from the Wendmouth or down the Hawk Road from the Hawkwold, around level 10, and the two roads meet at the one place the river can be waded. Two towns a long walk apart, a ford between them, a drowned weir and an old wood. Keep it wide and quiet, not busy.

## Premise
- **Where Two Roads Meet**: The Wend Road and the Hawk Road come in from opposite corners and end at the same ford. A company that took one has heard nothing of the other, and the folk here have an opinion of both. Whoever arrives first is asked, politely, which way they came. tags: premise, company, roads
- **The Only Wading Place**: The Wend can be crossed on foot nowhere else on this map, and the planks laid over the ford's two deepest tiles are older than the town. Everything between Aldermere and Brandhold passes the Weirgate Watch, and the Watch counts it. tags: premise, ford
- **The Water Has Been Moving**: The river that reaches the sea at the Wendmouth is lower at the ford than the old marks say, and the Drowned Weir, which was built to hold it, is no longer holding. The millers have noticed. The weir-keepers' oath is the thing nobody mentions. tags: premise, weir, river

## Threads
- **The Green Keeper**: A figure in a green cloak keeps the ford and gives travellers a receipt in a hand nobody can read. Gate-folk say it has done so since before the Watch. It asks nothing a company cannot pay, and it has never been seen to cross. tags: ford, keeper, act1
- **The Drowned Weir**: The sluice stair is open again and things come up it at night. The Watch has stopped sending men down. Aldermere's millers would pay for it shut, and cannot say why they are afraid of what is at the bottom. tags: weir, dungeon, act1
- **Thornback**: A boar grown huge in the old wood, with a back like a hedge, has been taking the fences of the south bank. Brandhold's herders call him the Boar-King and will not go into the wood after dusk. tags: thornback, elite
- **The Long Road East**: The Kilnway runs from the ford past the turning for Brandhold to the map's edge. Smiths come in from it with soot to their elbows and will not say where they have been. It is not open yet: an order to walk it is refused in words. Keep hinting that travellers set out on it and turn back, and never promise an arrival. tags: roads, act2
- **Two Towns, One Ford**: Aldermere keeps the north bank and Brandhold the south, and each says the other lets its sheep drink upstream. Sides here are old stories, not a border. tags: aldermere, brandhold, act1

## Characters
- **Gate Captain of Aldermere**: Hallowan, soft-spoken and exact. Wants the weir shut before the autumn market. Secret: the Watch's ledger of who crossed the ford has been written in two hands for some years. tags: aldermere, npc
- **Brandhold's Forge-Warden**: Ashfolk, heavy-handed, counts every ingot that leaves the yard. Wants the Kilnway kept clear and the charcoal honest. Secret: the yard is short of green stone, and has been since the weir stopped holding. tags: brandhold, npc
- **Weir-Warden Orsk**: A Grave Warden of the old weir-keepers, sitting in a chair in a pool in the dark. He took an oath to hold the river back and has held it a very long time. Not a villain; a thing that has not been relieved. tags: weir, orsk
- **The Green Keeper**: Not a person and not a creature: what is left of the weir-keepers' oath, walking the crossing it was made for. It is kind. It does not know that it is not alive. DM-only. tags: ford, keeper
- **Thornback**: The map's one elite, a boar the size of a cart. Keep him in the old wood until the company has seen what the wood's creatures can do. tags: thornback, elite

## Plans
- **Act I, levels 10-12, Wendreach and the ford**: The arrival by either road, Aldermere, the Weirgate Watch and the Green Keeper's receipt. Teach the width and the quiet: the towns do not know each other's news. tags: act1
- **Act II, levels 12-15, the Greywood and the Ashmarch**: The Drowned Weir, Thornback in the old wood, Brandhold and the road east. Let the company choose which hall it rests in first. tags: act2
- **If they came from the Wendmouth or the Hawkwold**: Whatever they carried is the only thing here that knows them: a name from a ledger, a toll receipt, a stranger's word. Let one thing from the road they took turn out to have been heard of here, and once. tags: roads
- **The Ways Back Stay Open**: Both roads run both ways. A company may go home; the lands behind will be as they left them, and they will have outgrown them. tags: roads
- **If the company falls**: Death is permanent. A replacement walks in at the next camp or town; a stranger from these parts, who has never heard of the roads behind. tags: hardcore

## Secrets
- **The Receipt**: The Green Keeper's receipts are in the weir-keepers' own tally-script. Set beside any mark cut into the Weir's green stone they read the same: each is a name and a count, and the count is of those who crossed and were not held. Where: the Sluice Stair, or any receipt a hero has kept. tags: ford, weir, reveal
- **The Two Hands**: The Watch's ledger is written in the captain's hand by day and another by night; the second hand is older than the captain. Where: the Weirgate Watch's ledger, or her own confession over a cup. tags: aldermere, ford, reveal
- **The Short Yard**: Brandhold has not had green stone for the forge-lintels for a long time, and the Forge-Warden has been buying it off a man who takes it from the Weir. Where: the yard's tally, or the man himself on the road to the ford. tags: brandhold, weir, reveal
