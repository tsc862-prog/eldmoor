# Campaign seed: Eldmoor (80x50)

DM-only. The heroes never read this; reveal it through narrate, npc_say, hooks, staged scenes and marks. Everything starts on the Fellowship's road. Names rolled for townsfolk are not canon; roles are.

## Premise
- **The Lanterns Are Going Out**: The flames on the Thornvale and Mirefen roadside lanternstones were lit long ago on purpose, and nobody now living remembers the purpose. They are guttering, one by one, outward from the crossroads. Where one fails, the dead and the old fires stir. Whoever keeps the road lit keeps the valley quiet. tags: premise, lanterns, road
- **Who Lit Them**: Ossian Vael, the Drowned King, lit the line from a spark of Kragmaw's own fire, then drowned his court in the Sunken Crypt under Lich-Warden Vesk to hold something else down. The living towns inherited the road, the shrines and the debt. The debt is coming due. tags: premise, drowned-king, debt

## Threads
- **The Dimming Shrines**: Wayside lanternstones in Thornvale gutter at dusk; a pilgrim swears one was lit yesterday and is dark today. Mark a shrine near the Fellowship's road and let it fail in front of them. Pull: place mark (shrine), danger on that road, a hook to relight it. tags: lanterns, thornvale, act1
- **The Rookscar Toll**: Rookscar crews wave through anyone bearing a Toll-Token and rob everyone else. Their toll is older than Highgate: they are collecting under the old road-law. Parley before you fight; a token in a Fellowship pack opens a different road. tags: rookscar, tokens, road
- **The Gate Captain's Ledger**: Highgate's chandlers swear their lamp-oil order has been the same for two hundred years, to the cask, for roads no one lights. Grimhold's gate warden keeps a matching tally of salt. Someone owes someone. tags: ledger, highgate, grimhold
- **Hollowfang Knows the Road**: The Old Wolf of Thornvale walks the same path every dusk, shrine to shrine, and the dark ones have fresh claw-marks. An early elite rumour with a reason behind it. tags: hollowfang, elite, act1
- **What the Fen Remembers**: Old Mother Sedge's nests clog reed beds that were once a king's causeway. A hermit in Mirefen has been walking its stones, counting them. tags: mirefen, sedge, act2
- **The Cold Door in Hoarfell**: The Cairn-Door Key opens nothing in any town. The Tarn Wights climb out earlier each dusk, and the Tarn-King wears a crown he does not remember earning. tags: hoarfell, key, act3

## Characters
- **Gate Captain of Highgate**: Concord gate post. Keeps the ledger of writs and notices the oil order is not shrinking. Dry, tired, fair; hands the Fellowship its first writ and its first doubt. Secret: has read the first page of the ledger and put it back. tags: highgate, npc, concord
- **Gate Warden of Grimhold**: Warband gate post. Blunt, counts bodies as cheerfully as salt sacks. Wants the Fellowship to come back, in that order of priority. Secret: the pit-lords paid for a locked chest nobody is allowed to open. tags: grimhold, npc, warband
- **The Priest and the Shaman**: Chapel of Highgate, spirit circle of Grimhold. Both light a candle for every name that leaves the gate and both have run out of the same wax. Each knows half a prayer for lanternstones. tags: highgate, grimhold, priest, shaman
- **Edrin Pask, Wagon Merchant**: A tinker with a lantern on the bow who sells road-worn gear and gossip, and is never where he said. Wants the road kept lit because a lit road is a paying road. Voice: cheerful, evasive. Secret: carries Candle-Stubs from every shrine, lit once. tags: peddler, road, thornvale
- **Black Tam Harrow**: Rookscar toll-reeve of the Mirefen road; a tall man in a rook-mask. Wants the toll honoured. Voice: courteous, never raises it. Secret: his crew are the last road-wardens of the Drowned King, and he holds the tally of every debt. tags: rookscar, mirefen, parley
- **Mother Ysmay, the Causeway Hermit**: Lives in a camp in Mirefen counting submerged stones. Wants someone to finish the count. Voice: sing-song, unhurried. Secret: she was a Crypt scribe's granddaughter; the count ends at the Crypt mouth. tags: hermit, mirefen, secret
- **Old Vrogg, Sellsword**: Warband veteran drifting the Mirefen road, selling his axe by the day. Wants a death worth the song. Voice: gravel, gallows jokes. Secret: he carries a half-burnt oath-knife older than Grimhold. tags: sellsword, warband, oath

## Plans
- **Act I, levels 1-5, Thornvale**: Make the lanterns the Fellowship's quiet puzzle. Wolves at the gate, a failing shrine, a Toll-Token. Do not name the Drowned King yet. End when a Fellowship member kneels at a shrine that goes out under their hands (shrine mark, then a lever for danger on that stretch). tags: act1
- **Act II, levels 6-12, Mirefen and the Sunken Crypt**: The Rookscar parley, the causeway hermit, Old Mother Sedge, then the Crypt. Let Vesk's court be a sorrow, not only a boss: the drowned are keeping an oath. The Crypt run should feel earned and ask what the Fellowship will carry out. tags: act2
- **Act III, levels 13-20, Cinderpass, Hoarfell, Kragmaw**: The lanterns burn from Kragmaw's spark. The raid is a choice about what follows: Kept Light (a hero carries a flame home), Dark Road (the line dies), or Unowned Flame (it burns white and answers to no one). Hoarfell's Tarn-King answers the Cairn-Door Key. tags: act3
- **Opening beats, days 1-4**: Day 1: the gate captain's wolves writ and the first dark shrine seen from the road. Day 2: Edrin Pask appears with a Shrine Candle-Stub; log_intent. Day 3: a hostile or wary Rookscar crew parley, a Toll-Token offered. Day 4: Hollowfang rumoured at the inn, claw-marks at dark shrines. If a member dies, the oath and any held token pass to the replacement and the Chronicler names the loss. tags: openings
- **Concord Fellowship**: Hear it first from the chapel: the candle-wax is the shared prayer. The ledger of Highgate holds the first page. Hallowans tell it as debt; Tarrows and Skerrins as duty. Concord voice stays formal. tags: concord, adapt
- **Warband Fellowship**: Hear it first at the spirit circle: the salt tally and the locked chest. Ashfolk tell it as a bargain they refuse to break; the pit-lords will not admit it. Grimhold's gate warden is the opening contact. tags: warband, adapt

## Prologue
- **The first delve**: The company's first dungeon is a small one near its hall: read `first_delve` in dm_status for the place. Keep it small and human, a chore that turns out to be real. Hint at the campaign's premise through one object or sight below, no more: this is where they learn each other's voices, and the world's real danger comes after.

## Secrets
- **The Lanterns Are Leashes**: They keep the dead on the Lantern Road asleep, and the flame is borrowed from Kragmaw. Where: a Lore roll at a failing shrine (mystery mark with cast.reveal), or Edrin Pask's Candle-Stubs. tags: lanterns, reveal
- **Vesk Is a Keeper, Not a Usurper**: The Lich-Warden holds the oath the Drowned King made; the court is not hostile by choice. Where: Mother Ysmay's causeway count ends at the Crypt mouth; a Table scene at the Crypt with the Warden's voice (npc_say or stage_scene meeting). tags: vesk, crypt
- **The Rookscar Are the Debt**: Black Tam's toll is the old wardens' pay, kept by tradition. Where: a parley that succeeds, or a staged meeting after a Fellowship member is ambushed. tags: rookscar, tam
- **The Wyrm Owns the Spark**: Kragmaw's Molten Eye is the lens of the mother-flame. Where: the Cairn-Door Key opens a cairn door in Hoarfell; behind it, the old wardens' tally, and the choice. tags: kragmaw, key
