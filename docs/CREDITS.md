# Credits

Third-party art used in Eldmoor (owner's decision on #789: CC0 and LPC sets may be used, with credit). Everything else in
`assets/` is generated by the project's own pipeline (`scripts/art/`) or drawn in code (`src/render/`). The sets are fetched
by `npm run art:packs` into `assets/packs/<set>/`, which is gitignored: only the pieces the game uses are committed, each with
`source` and `license` on its `assets/index.json` entry.

## LPC base terrain

`tile:grass`, `tile:road`, `tile:water` and the `grass-road` and `bank-water` transition pieces are cut from the LPC base
tiles `grass.png`, `dirt.png` and `water.png` by Leana Zimmerman (Sharm), CC-BY-SA 3.0 (also GPL 3.0 and OGA-BY 3.0), from
the 2012 Liberated Pixel Cup base assets (https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles).
The cut pieces are derivative works and stay under CC-BY-SA 3.0. The script is `scripts/art/packcut.mjs`.
