# Credits

Third-party art used in Eldmoor (owner's decision on #789: CC0 and LPC sets may be used, with credit). Everything else in
`assets/` is generated by the project's own pipeline (`scripts/art/`) or drawn in code (`src/render/`). The sets are fetched
by `npm run art:packs` into `assets/packs/<set>/`, which is gitignored: only the pieces the game uses are committed, each with
`source` and `license` on its `assets/index.json` entry.

## LPC base terrain

`tile:grass`, `tile:road`, `tile:water` and the `grass-road` and `bank-water` transition pieces are cut from the LPC base
tiles `grass.png`, `dirt.png` and `water.png` by Leana Zimmerman (Sharm), CC-BY-SA 3.0 (also GPL 3.0 and OGA-BY 3.0), from
the 2012 Liberated Pixel Cup base assets (https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles).
The cut pieces are derivative works and stay under CC-BY-SA 3.0. The script is `scripts/art/packcut.mjs`.

## LPC landmarks

`landmark:crypt-mouth` is cut from the arch and brick course of `castlewalls.png` by Daniel Armstrong (HughSpectrum) and `landmark:lair` from
the charcoal rock of `lavarock.png` by Leana Zimmerman (Sharm), both from the 2012 Liberated Pixel Cup base assets, CC-BY-SA 3.0 (also GPL 3.0).
The stone is recoloured onto the crypt's violet-grey and a few details are drawn by hand; the pieces are derivative works and stay under CC-BY-SA 3.0.
The script is `scripts/art/landmarkcut.mjs`.
## LPC rocks and trees

`prop:rock-<biome>-1..3` are cut from `rock.png` and `prop:canopy-<biome>-1..3` (whole trees: crown over trunk and ground shadow) from `treetop.png`
and `trunk.png`, all by Leana Zimmerman (Sharm), CC-BY-SA 3.0 (also GPL 3.0 and OGA-BY 3.0), from the same base assets. Pixels are
LPC's at 1:1; per biome (meadow, fen, ash, tundra) they are only recoloured, and the tundra firs get a dusting of snow. Derivative
works under CC-BY-SA 3.0. The script is `scripts/art/packcut.mjs props`.

## LPC hero sheets

`sprite:hero-<class>-<people>` (seven classes by the six peoples of `src/data/races.js`) and the class fallback sheets
`sprite:hero-<class>` are composed by `scripts/art/lpc-heroes.mjs` from the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator's
layers (choices in `scripts/art/lpc-heroes.json`), the tabard layer recoloured to the faction colours. Each sheet is a derivative
work and is shared under CC-BY-SA 3.0, as the share-alike layers require. Every layer also keeps its own licences, which are the
generator's `CREDITS.csv` rows for its layer folder: a line below says what the layer is offered under and which of those it is
used under. The arming sword (`weapon/sword/arming`, ElizaWy and JaidynReiman) is offered only under OGA-BY 3.0 and is used under
that licence (attribution), not under CC-BY-SA; the same goes for any other layer whose line says "used under OGA-BY 3.0" or CC0.
