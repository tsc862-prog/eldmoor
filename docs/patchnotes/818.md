---
title: The DM keeps its words when a hero falls
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [655, 817]
---
- When a hero goes down, the DM's own line now reaches you even if its voice was busy with something else; before, a plain scripted line often took its place.
- If a downed hero is saved while the DM is still describing the fall, you now hear both moments in the DM's words.
