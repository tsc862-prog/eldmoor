---
title: The Vale's river has a sea and a spring
date: 2026-11-05
area: world
issues: [940]
---
- The Wend now widens into a sea at the western edge of the Vale, and Gullstrand's quay runs out on a timber pier into the water.
- At its head the river rises from a spring pool under the rock instead of simply stopping.
- Only three places cross the river now: the Low Bridge, the Weirgate ford and the High Bridge; a stray road bridge beside the ford is gone.
- Rimecrown's ice pools are rounder, the farm fields near Wendmouth have ragged edges, and the braziers, the Kiln and Ashenfold's embers glow at night.
