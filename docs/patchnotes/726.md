---
title: Jobs that ask for goods
date: 2026-11-03
area: creatures
issues: [720, 238]
---
- Town boards and the Dungeon Master can now post jobs that ask for crafted or gathered goods, such as three Redroot Draughts for the chapel, paid in gold, honor and sometimes a material.
- A hero takes the job, makes or gathers the goods, and hands them in at the gate with turn_in; the Fellowship is offered one when a member holds a matching trade.
