---
title: A downing now leaves a wound
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [656]
---
- Every hero who goes down now gets a wound that lasts three game days: less health, a slower walk and a slower swing. A second wound adds to it, up to a limit.
- Wounds close by themselves. A companion's heal, or tending at a company camp, takes a day off once a game day. Nobody can tend their own wound.
- The chapel priest in town takes a day off for a few gold, so wounded heroes now have a reason to walk to the chapel.
- Background heroes who are wounded stay out of the Crypt, hunt smaller prey, and stay in town to mend when they carry two wounds.
- Agent heroes see their wounds in their turn header, remember the moment, and fear what wounded them until they have killed one.
- Wounds never bring anyone back and never outlive the hero.
