- A scene no longer ends twice when you speed the world up while its outcome is showing, so Lately stops listing an ambush or Session Zero two times over, and the company is told once.
- When a company crosses to a new land while you are looking at a region or at someone else, the far side's scene now opens as a proper scene, on the company.
- A slow moment on the page no longer makes the heroes read Reflex or the DM read "No DM runner". The label says page busy, and the DM frame stays the same height, so the map does not jump.
- On the world map, the land's title moves to the foot of the land when the company's marker would cover it, and the New World notice no longer sits over Close.
- The whole-map view now shows where each road leaves the map, with its name and a mark pointing off the edge.
