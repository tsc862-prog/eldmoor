title: Table scenes are answered every round
date: 2026-11-05
area: infra
issues: [826]

- Your companions' minds now answer a Table scene in every round and when the same proposal is put again; before, a repeat could go unanswered for minutes while the world waited.
- A companion whose turn at the table has not come no longer has its mind woken again and again with nothing to do, which saves model calls.
