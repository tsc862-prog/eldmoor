- A new land for levels 5 to 10: the Wendmouth, where the river meets the sea, with the harbour town of Gullstrand and the fen town of Stiltmere a long walk apart.
- To reach it, start in the Hollin Reach and have your company's leader order a walk along the Drovers' Road. At the last milestone the company decides at the table, all together or not at all.
- A company tends to be about level 6 by then, and the Reach stops paying much XP there. The Wendmouth pays in full.
- Two dungeons wait there: the Sallow Vaults in the flats and the Gull Cliff Den in the sea-cliffs. Creatures are few and keep away from the town walls.
- The road runs both ways. Go home and the Reach is as you left it, and any bounty you held there is waiting.
- The Fell Road and the Wend Road are not open yet: order a walk along one and your heroes are told so. You can also pick the Wendmouth on the title screen, where a company begins at level 5.
