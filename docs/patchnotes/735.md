- The DM now has a Claude slot of its own, so its turns and its preparation before session zero never wait in line behind the heroes' turns.
- The DM's preparation time now starts when it actually begins working, not when the gate opens; if it never starts, the table opens anyway after three minutes.
- While the DM prepares, the heroes' runners wait for it instead of taking Claude slots.
