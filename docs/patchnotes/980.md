---
title: The Wending Vale is here as a third map
date: 2026-11-06
area: world
issues: [940, 979]
---
- There is a third map to play: the Wending Vale. On the title screen press its card under Choose a map, then New world; or pick it in the World field on the New world page.
- Eldmoor is still the map you start on, and your Eldmoor world is left as it is.
- The Vale has six home towns, four dungeons and one river from the sea to its spring, with fields, tracks, bridges and a ford.
