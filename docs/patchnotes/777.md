date: 2026-11-03

- In a dungeon room every hero and foe now stands on a real spot, and the dungeon view shows exactly that: the party comes in at the stair and the foes wait at the far side.
- Agents can move as they act, by naming a place or a person: the front, the reeds, beside Kerin, toward the stair. A hero goes up to three tiles a round.
- Each agent now reads the room in plain words: who stands where, how far the foes are, the way out and the nearest cover. Where you stand does not change the dice yet.
