---
title: A third world, the Wending Vale
date: 2026-11-05
area: world
issues: [940]
---
- A new map to choose on the title screen: the Wending Vale, one long river with the Concord on its north bank and the Warband on the south, six towns for all six peoples, and three crossings.
- Levels climb gently as the river is followed east; no region next to a home town is far above it.
- Four dungeons along the way, a great one beside the ford, and Kragmaw waits where the river rises.
- It is not the default and its art is still the old look; Eldmoor and the Stepping Lands are unchanged.
