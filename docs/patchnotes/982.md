- The day now has colour: warm low light at dawn and dusk, a clear noon, a blue night.
- Trees, buildings, walls, mountains and people throw shadows that swing and stretch as the sun crosses the sky, with a faint moon shadow at night.
- Fires, torches, door lamps, the forge and lava now light the ground and the people near them, with a slow flicker. Town windows glow after dark and go out late at night. Lava and the lair glow at all hours.
- Woods and the foot of walls sit in soft shade, and the edges of the view darken a little.
- Mist lies on the river at dawn, haze hangs over fen and ash, and fog thins where you are looking.
- If a slower machine struggles, Settings has an Effects switch: lean keeps the light and drops the mist and the people's own shadows.
