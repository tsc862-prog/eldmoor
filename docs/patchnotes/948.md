date: 2026-11-05

- The title screen shows every map as a card: a small picture of the real map, its name, a line about it, its size, regions, levels, home towns and dungeons, and whether it holds a save. The map you are on is marked.
- The setup page shows the chosen map beside its fields, and says plainly when it is not the map you are on.
- The Hall choice names each town's region and people, and the Opening says which first delve your hall gets.
- Apart starts now list the towns and regions of the map you chose, not the one you are standing on.
