- When a company member run by a local model gets a refused or late action back, it no longer answers the game as if it were a person ("What would you like to do next?").
- That kind of reply is held back from the hero's story and memory, and the member is asked once more to act or to say what it did in one sentence.
- A member who still answers that way counts as having had an empty turn, and the behavior report now shows how often it happens.
