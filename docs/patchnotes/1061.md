---
title: "No more standing on the riverbank: fights with a foe out of reach end"
date: 2026-11-07
area: ai
issues: [1060]
---
- A company no longer stands in a fight for an hour with its foe on the far side of a river with no crossing: the fight ends at once and they hunt on.
- A creature that comes for a camp at night now always comes from the camp's own side of the water.
- If your hero is ordered to fight something it cannot reach, it says so ("Your foe is out of reach") instead of waiting on the bank.
- A creature chasing a hero across water it cannot cross gives up and goes home.
