---
title: The Wending Vale's towns get their own shapes
date: 2026-11-05
area: world
issues: [940]
---
- Each of the six Vale towns now has its own layout instead of the same square plan: Aldermere around a market green, Gullstrand as a harbour with a quay, Hawkstead on terraces, Brandhold as a forge street, Stiltmere wide and loose, Hornridge as a hold on a long slope.
- Towns have paved streets and lanes inside the walls and approaches outside them.
- Townsfolk no longer start their day inside a building.
