---
title: Raid sites and sites for everyone
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [642]
---
- The DM can now raise raid sites: five or more heroes go down, the boss gets fiercer as it weakens, and after a kill the place lies quiet for two days before its keeper returns.
- The world's own DM now raises dungeon sites by itself after a cleared lair, a recovered relic or a migration or invasion, and ordinary heroes form parties to go down into them.
- A site's name in a hero's Log and in the timeline opens its dungeon view.
