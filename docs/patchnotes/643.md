---
title: Generated adventure sites
date: 2026-11-03
area: world
issues: [69]
---
- The DM can place new dungeon sites in a region (barrow, fortress, temple, mine, den or ruin) with 3 to 8 rooms, a named boss and a guaranteed epic. Heroes find them by walking close.
- A solo hero can go down alone; a company votes and enters together, and the crawl plays room by room like the Crypt. Cleared sites leave a ruin; uncleared ones grow harder and eventually collapse.
