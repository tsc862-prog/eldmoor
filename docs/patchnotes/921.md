---
title: "DM-made dungeons keep clear of two names too close to real places"
date: 2026-11-05
area: lore
issues: [919]
---
- A DM-made dungeon can no longer be called Rimereach or Oldmoor, which read too much like Rimeholt and Eldmoor.
- The names of DM-made dungeons and their rooms are now written down in the lore.
