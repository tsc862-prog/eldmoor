---
title: The DM no longer speaks for your heroes
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [750]
---
- On a quiet night round the fire, the fire itself now puts a question to one hero, who may answer it; no companion is made to ask it.
- If the DM ever writes words into a hero's mouth, that line is cut before anyone hears it, and the DM is told that heroes speak for themselves.
