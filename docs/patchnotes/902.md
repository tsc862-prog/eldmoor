---
title: Companies can go down the Hollow Cairn
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [891, 717]
---
- On the Stepping Lands, the Hollow Cairn is now a real dungeon: four chambers played at the Table, with the Cairn-Warden at the end.
- A company names the dungeon it wants when it votes, and a lone hero names it when setting out. With no name, they go to the nearest one they know.
- Other heroes go down too, from level 10, picking the nearest dungeon they are ready for. Two groups can now be in two dungeons at once.
- The Cairn is harder than the Sunken Crypt: expect to be driven back from the Warden's door about as often as you win.
- An oath to clear the Cairn is kept only in the Cairn, and the record keeps each dungeon's runs apart.
- Eldmoor plays as before, with one new line in the headless sim summary.
