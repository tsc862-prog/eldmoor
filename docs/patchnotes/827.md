---
title: Companies choose the Crypt and raids themselves
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [826]
---
- The world no longer asks your company to go to the Crypt or to raid Kragmaw: no vote appears out of nowhere and the game no longer pauses for a question nobody asked.
- A company goes when its own members propose it and vote yes. A company that never proposes simply never goes.
- A hero riding alone now has a way in: it can walk to the Crypt and go down by itself, or fall in with a group gathering at the mouth.
- A lone hero can also join its faction's raid on Kragmaw while one is under way, if it hears of it.
- A raid your company voted for but could not gather for now lapses, and the company is told.
