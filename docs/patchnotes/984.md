- Rivers flow: glints run with the current, foam works at the banks, the ford and bridge piers, and rings spread where someone wades.
- Cloud shadows drift across the land by day.
- Wind walks through the world: crowns sway, grass leans and wheat ripples in gusts, harder in a storm.
- The air has life by region: leaves near woods, fireflies from dusk, gnats over the fen, embers and ash on burnt ground, snow over the tundra. Chimney smoke trails away downwind.
- In the Wending Vale, bridges, fields, wildflowers and lilies now always appear; before, they could stay missing.
- The Effects switch in Settings turns the moving things off on a slower machine.
