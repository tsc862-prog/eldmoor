---
title: The raid view tells the truth about the fallen
date: 2026-11-06
area: render
issues: [85]
---
- A raider who drops at Kragmaw's lair now lies where they fell, with their death saves showing, instead of being listed as fled. If they die it counts as a fall, and if they get up it says so.
- The lair in the raid view has the same dark rock nest as the world map, and heroes are drawn twice as large, so you can pick them out at a glance.
- When Kragmaw is slain the lava in the raid view cools to black rock.
- A company's fight with Kragmaw at the table now opens the raid view by itself, and the table banner has a View the raid button.
- Any creature caught in the lair is listed on the bar under the raid view, beside the raiders.
