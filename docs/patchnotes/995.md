---
title: Mountains that look like mountains
date: 2026-11-06
area: render
issues: [989]
---
- Mountains are now real ranges: every peak has its own shape, with ridges, gullies and scree, and the near ones stand in front of the far ones.
- The foot of a range wanders over the land, with loose stones around it, instead of following square tiles.
- Snow caps follow height: high peaks in mild country, whole ranges in the cold north, and none on the ash.
- Where two regions meet on a mountain, the rock and snow now change slowly, and the pale broken cliff pieces are gone.
- The sun lights one side of a range and leaves the other in shade, moving through the day, and tall peaks throw long shadows at dusk.
