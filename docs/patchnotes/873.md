---
title: The follow view shows more of the world around your heroes
date: 2026-11-04
area: render
issues: [846]
---
- On a wide screen the follow view now shows about 40 tiles across, so you see the road, the town and the other heroes around the one you follow. Small windows keep the old closer view.
- The pixel art stays sharp at the new zoom, and the plus and minus buttons still work.
- In the dungeon, the room being fought in and the corridor before it now fill the pane instead of leaving half of it black.
