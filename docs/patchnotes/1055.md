- A new land for levels 10 to 15: the Weirgate, old meadow and wood round the one place the Wend can be waded, with the market town of Aldermere on the north bank and, a long walk east, the forge town of Brandhold.
- Reach it by the Wend Road from the Wendmouth or the Hawk Road from the Hawkwold: the road at the far end of each is open now, and your company's leader can order the walk and decide at the table.
- The Drowned Weir waits beside the ford, a five-room dungeon of green stone with a warden at the bottom, and Thornback, a boar the size of a cart, stalks the old wood. Creatures are few and keep well away from the town walls.
- The Kilnway at the east edge is not open yet: order a walk along it and your heroes are told so. You can also pick the Weirgate on the title screen, where a company begins at level 10.
