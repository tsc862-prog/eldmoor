---
title: "Companies can reach far dungeons on the Wending Vale"
date: 2026-11-06
area: world
issues: [1002, 1003]
---
- On the Wending Vale a company or guild that votes for a dungeon far from its hall now has time to walk there, instead of turning back on the road. This is the Drowned Weir from either home bank.
- A new world's first Chronicle lines name the world's own dungeon, the region of Kragmaw's lair and the world itself, instead of Eldmoor's.
