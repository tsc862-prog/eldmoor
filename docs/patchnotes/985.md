---
title: The Vale's land loses its squares
date: 2026-11-06
area: render
issues: [979]
---
- On the Wending Vale, ice pools are round, lava lies in flows and the fen has curved banks, where they used to be stairs of squares.
- Region borders wander like real country, and mountains no longer sit in pale squares on the dark ash.
- Bridges, Gullstrand's jetty, fields and flowers could go missing on the Vale, so a road ran into the water; they now always appear.
- Flowers grow as single blossoms instead of square patches.
