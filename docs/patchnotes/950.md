---
title: Tools stop breaking on odd input
date: 2026-11-05
area: infra
issues: []
---
- A new test calls every tool a hero or the DM has with sensible, empty and nonsense input in many situations, and the odd cases it found are fixed.
- Starting a festival or other world event on the very first day no longer fails to post its jobs.
- A creature, place or recipe named with odd words no longer breaks the call or sends a hero walking to nowhere.
- A refused tool call now says what is missing instead of printing "undefined" or "NaN".
