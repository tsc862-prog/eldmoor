title: Heroes swear oaths
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [526]
---
- Heroes can now swear a vow on purpose (slay, recover, reach, protect or free) with companions in earshot as witnesses. It appears in the quest log.
- Renouncing a vow costs bonds and is written in the chronicle; a broken word becomes a DM thread. Kept vows are remembered.
