---
title: Art generation runs by itself
date: 2026-11-03
area: infra
issues: [661]
---
The launcher now also runs the art generator in the background: it makes images until the day's share of the monthly credits is spent or the provider says stop, then waits a few hours and checks again by itself.
Days that make nothing add their share to the following days of the month, so the whole allowance gets used, and the month's total is never passed.
Press s in the launcher to see when it checks next and how much is spent; start the launcher with --no-art to leave it off.
