date: 2026-11-03

- The DM gets simple tools for the remaining world levers: retiring a person, withdrawing a job, changing attitudes and conditions, price shocks, danger areas, events, relations, custom foes and sites.
- The DM can also undo its last move within a few game hours, dismiss its own markers, or cancel a staged scene that has not opened yet.
