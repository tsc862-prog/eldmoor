- A fifth and last land for levels 14 and up: the Wend's Head, at the end of the Kilnway from the Weirgate. One town, Kilnmark, a long walk of slag and scree to the old kilns, and further on a cold crown where the Wend rises from its spring pool.
- The Brandwright Kiln waits in the Kilnlands, four chambers with a clay warden at the heart, and Grimbrand, a kiln-golem gone wild, walks the brickyards. Creatures are few and keep well away from the town wall.
- Kragmaw sleeps in its ring of lava beyond the pool, and this is the only land where it wakes: a company's own raid can ride there. Here the wyrm is twice as big and hits half again as hard as in Eldmoor, so a level 16 company wins about four times in ten and a level 18 to 20 company about six, and a raid that goes badly breaks off.
- The Kilnway from the Weirgate is open now: your company's leader can order the walk and decide at the table. There is no road beyond the Head. You can also pick the Wend's Head on the title screen, where a company begins at level 14.
