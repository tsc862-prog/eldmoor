---
title: Crafted goods at the auction house
date: 2026-11-03
area: creatures
issues: [719, 238, 13]
---
- Potions, packs and other things a recipe makes can now be listed at the auction house, and heroes who are short of potions will buy them.
- Crafted gear keeps its maker's name through a sale, and the listing tells you who made it.
