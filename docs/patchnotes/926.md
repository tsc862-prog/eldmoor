title: A new company starts with a first dungeon
date: 2026-11-05
area: ai
issues: [922]
---
- A new world now opens at the mouth of a small dungeon near your hall: the company meets there, introduces itself, then goes down room by room.
- When they come out they stand at the hall gate, the gate captain has a warning for them, and the leader gets the first move as before.
- It is easy but not safe: heroes can go down and can die, and a lost seat is refilled the usual way. It ends around level 2, with plain loot.
- The Opening field on the setup page turns it off: pick Straight to the hall.
- Heroes who start alone keep their short opening, and worlds you already have are not touched.
- The cleared dungeon stays on the map as a ruin the company can remember.
