date: 2026-11-05
area: ai

- Heroes of level 3 and up who wear no guild may hear at the town gate that banners of their side still take new names, at most once every few game days. It is only a word; nothing is joined for them.
- A hero can now leave a guild with a new tool and later join another or raise their own.
