---
title: "Town gates are gatehouses now"
date: 2026-11-06
area: render
issues: [979]
---
- Every town gate has a gatehouse instead of a gap in the wall: towers with the town's banners, and on north and south walls an arch with a raised portcullis that people walk through.
- Gate guards stand either side of the road, outside the towers.
- Town walls have more depth along their tops: laid slabs and battlements that throw a little shade.
- Great halls, chapels and gatehouses now throw longer shadows across the town at dawn and dusk.
