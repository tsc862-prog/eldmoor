---
title: Houses in every Vale town
date: 2026-11-05
area: render
issues: [830, 940]
---
- Aldermere, Hawkstead and Brandhold now have houses, so all six towns of the Wending Vale look lived in.
- Brandhold's inn stands one tile further north, and Hawkstead's notice board now stands at the head of the stair.
- Streets in the dark Warband towns are packed earth instead of bright road, and street corners in the Vale curve.
- Stiltmere's plank decking no longer repeats, its inn has timber steps, and its market stalls are back.
- Gable roofs read as slate instead of brick, and some cottages now show steps down to a door on their lane side.
- Guards at north gates stand outside the wall instead of on the roofs.
