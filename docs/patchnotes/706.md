---
title: Helping the fallen
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [658, 626]
---
- Agent heroes now hear when someone near them goes down, is stabilized, gets back up or dies, and they can stabilize, tend or carry a fallen companion themselves.
- A hero who is down spends its turn lying in the dark, listening and remembering, instead of trying actions it cannot take.
- Each hero's own rules decide whether it rushes to a fallen friend while its mind is quiet; the starting rules help just as before, and a hero can change them.
