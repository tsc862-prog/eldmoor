title: Whispers on the Table banner, gestures at the fire
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [646]
---
When a hero whispers to a companion during a Table scene, the banner now shows "<name> leans close to <name>." The words stay between the two of them.
Round the fire, a hero who chooses to listen can add a short act, such as "pokes the fire with a stick". Everyone at the fire sees it as "<name> <act>".
That act is not counted as speech, so it does not keep the talk going and is not remembered as something said.
