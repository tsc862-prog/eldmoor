- Gullstrand's waterfront is now a small harbour: a plank quay along the bank either side of the jetty, boats riding at their moorings, crates, barrels, nets drying on racks and mooring posts on the edge.
- Two lamps at the jetty's mouth light the quay at night.
- Any town with a jetty gets the same treatment by itself. It changes only how the place looks: heroes walk where they did before.
