- A new land for levels 6 to 11: the Hawkwold, high open country above the Wend, with the falconers' town of Hawkstead and, a long walk away across a bridge, the hold at Hornridge.
- To reach it, start in the Hollin Reach and have your company's leader order a walk along the Fell Road. At the last milestone the company decides at the table, all together or not at all.
- Two dungeons wait there: the Herders' Cairn on a ridge in the west and the Kestrel Barrow in the hills east of Hawkstead. Creatures are few and keep well away from the town walls.
- The road runs both ways. Go home and the Reach is as you left it.
- The Hawk Road at the far edge is not open yet: order a walk along it and your heroes are told so. You can also pick the Hawkwold on the title screen, where a company begins at level 6.
