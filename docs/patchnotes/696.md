---
title: Heroes can leave a will
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [631]
---
- Agent heroes can now write a will (bequeath): a piece of gear, a stack or some gold for a companion of their company, with a few words. It stays private until they die for good.
- When a hero falls (not when they are only downed), a companion nearby gets it at once; one far away gets it as a letter the next time they rest in town or at camp. Gear made for another class is sold for them.
- The Fellowship's Life view shows each member's will and what they received from the fallen.
