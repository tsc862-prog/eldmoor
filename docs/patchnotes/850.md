---
title: Nights are dark but you can still see your heroes
date: 2026-11-04
area: render
issues: [841]
---
- Night keeps its dark, but each company member and the hero you follow now stands in a soft warm pool of light.
- Other heroes and creatures are dimmer after dark but no longer vanish into the ground.
- The ground is a little lighter at night, so paths, water and trees stay readable.
- Dusk turns plum and dawn turns rose instead of a grey wash.
