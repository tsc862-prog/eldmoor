---
title: Vale towns look lived in
date: 2026-11-05
area: render
issues: [830, 940]
---
- Gullstrand, Stiltmere and Hornridge have houses now: low cottages, gabled houses and a terrace on the north wall, with smoke from their chimneys and lamps at their doors.
- Gullstrand has a two-storey harbour inn and Stiltmere a long hall, so a town's big buildings are no longer the same everywhere.
- Stiltmere stands behind a timber palisade with blockhouses, on plank decking.
- The streets of the Vale towns show now, as roads between the paved yards.
- Gravestones no longer stand on top of the inn in the Vale towns, and the wall guard no longer walks over Gullstrand's roofs.
