---
title: "Elites hit as hard at the Table as they do in the world"
date: 2026-11-06
area: ai
issues: [1056]
---
- An elite fought at the Table is now a real danger: at the level you first meet one, about half the fights see someone go down. Fights in the world were already like this; the Table was far softer.
- Armour now counts once against an elite at the Table, so a blow that gets through lands in full. Wards and abilities still soften it.
- Elites use their tricks in rounds: Greymane opens with a howl that can stun the company, Old Mother Sedge hatches kin who join the fight, and a gore throws a hero back across the ground.
- An elite facing a full company can strike twice in a round, and it slows down as your numbers fall.
- Against an elite, a healer's plain heal now needs a rest of three rounds, unless someone is lying down.
- An elite's fight now runs up to 20 rounds at the Table, so it ends there rather than out in the world. Expect longer fights. Potions matter: a company carrying them is rarely downed.
