---
title: "The Wending Vale is wider, and its towns stand apart"
date: 2026-11-06
area: world
issues: [979]
---
- The Wending Vale is 40 columns wider. Aldermere, Gullstrand and Hawkstead now sit along the north bank with fields and open road between them, and Brandhold, Stiltmere and Hornridge are spread along the south bank, so the camera rarely shows two towns at once.
- The river, its three crossings, every dungeon, the wyrm's lair and all the names are the same; they have only moved east to make room.
- The ground just outside each town wall is far quieter: wild creatures no longer spawn right at the gates, so a fresh level-1 hero can step out and live.
- A Vale world saved before this change cannot continue on the wider map. The title screen says so instead of loading it; you can still export that save from the title screen, and a new Vale world starts normally. Worlds on the other two maps are not affected.
