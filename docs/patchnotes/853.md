- Timber Wolves, Rimehounds, both boars, Webspinners, Fen Lurkers, the Cinder Golem, Boglings and the Tarn Wight now walk as proper painted creatures that match the new heroes.
- They keep a believable size next to a hero: wolves and boars about hero height, the golem a head taller.
- The Ash Drake and the Tundra Shaghorn have no matching art yet and keep their old look for now.
