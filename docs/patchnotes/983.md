---
title: The Vale's towns are towns now
date: 2026-11-06
area: render
issues: [979]
---
- On the Wending Vale, every town is rebuilt at people's scale: houses, halls and chapels stand at least twice a hero's height, with timber upper floors, dormers and tall chimneys.
- Towns have streets, a square and a fountain, and their walls step in and out with a tower at every corner instead of being a plain box.
- Gullstrand's south gate now opens straight onto its quay.
- Fixed on every map: town walls and towers could go missing until something else made the map redraw.
