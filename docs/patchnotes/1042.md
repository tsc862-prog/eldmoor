---
title: The screen follows the road
date: 2026-11-06
area: render
issues: [1013]
---
- When a company takes a road to another land, the picture goes dark from the road's end, the new land's name stands on black, and the far side comes up where the company stands.
- There is no reload and nobody waits on the world: the map, minimap, region list and weather all change to the new land, in the cinema view too.
- A bar over the map names the road a company is heading for and what it is waiting on, such as someone out of shouting range.
- The title screen says which land your save stands on. No map in the game has an open road yet, so you will first see all this when the next land is ready.
