- Google's Gemini can now play heroes, whole companies and the DM, and answer the AI decisions, beside Claude, local models, OpenAI and Hugging Face.
- To turn it on, create a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey, add it as GOOGLE_API_KEY next to your other API keys, restart the game window, then pick a Gemini model in a mind row or the Start runner picker.
- On a free Google account every Gemini call costs nothing; when the day's free quota is used up the hero plays by the scripted rules (or the runner waits) until it resets at midnight Pacific time.
