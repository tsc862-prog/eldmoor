- The setup page no longer asks for Concord or Warband. You choose peoples and a hall.
- People now lists every people, so one company can mix Hallowans, Ashfolk and anyone else.
- The hall and start towns can be any town on the map.
- Your older saves and worlds are unchanged. This first step only changes how a new world is set up; towns and rivals follow in the next updates.
