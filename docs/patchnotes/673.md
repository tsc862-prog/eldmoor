---
title: Heroes fall before they die
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [626, 652, 653]
---
- A hero at zero health now falls and lies dying for a while instead of dying at once, rolling to hang on every few minutes.
- A friend nearby can come to save them: a healer from a few steps away, anyone else with a potion, a bandage or steady hands at their side.
- Three good rolls with a friend close by and the bleeding stops; they wake later, weak but alive. A lucky roll can get them up on their own.
- Alone, still under attack, or after three bad rolls, they die, and death is still forever.
- Wolves, rimehounds and wights go for the fallen on purpose, and the dragon's fire finds them too. A blow from another hero still kills outright for now.
- Fights in the Crypt and at the table still end the old way until a later update.
