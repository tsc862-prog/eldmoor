---
title: Fights in the open now play on the real ground
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [746]
---
- When your heroes fight an elite, an ambush, a rescue or Kragmaw, the ground around them counts: the scene map shows the patch they fight on, tile by tile.
- Heroes must get next to a foe to hit it, archers need a clear line, and flanking, guarding and free blows work as they do in the crypt.
- Trees and rocks are cover, snow and swamp slow you down, nobody crosses a river bank in one round, and shots still fly over water.
- Heroes can name the road back, the road, the bridge, the bank or the trees as they move, and a hero who falls back is really down the road when the fight ends.
- Foes walk toward you too, and the DM can bring an ambush in from an edge of the ground or along the road.
