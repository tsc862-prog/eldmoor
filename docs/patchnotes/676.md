title: Inside the DM's dungeons
date: 2026-11-03
area: render
issues: [642]
---
- A dungeon the DM raises now has its own inside view: barrows, keeps, temples, mines, dens and ruins each look and are furnished their own way.
- While a company crawls one you see which chamber they are in, the foes still standing and who is hurt, as in the Sunken Crypt.
- With nobody inside, the view tells whether the place is still hidden, was found, cleared or has fallen in; click its mouth on the map to look inside.
