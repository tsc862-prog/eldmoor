---
title: Log tab no longer says the agent ordered your level-ups
date: 2026-11-03
area: ui
issues: [785]
---
- Lines in a hero's Log such as reaching a level, a kill, loot, a fall or a new friend no longer carry the "agent order" tag, since nobody ordered them.
- Lines for things the hero decided, such as a road, a job or a sale, keep their tag.
