title: The DM speaks up
date: 2026-11-06
area: brain
issues: [1017]
---
- The DM now sets every scene your company sits down to: votes, fights, ambushes at camp, forks in a job and dungeon rooms, not only the opening and the fire.
- A fireside after the first scene no longer falls back to the stock lines: the DM's voice was skipping it by mistake.
- You will hear the narrator between scenes: as the company reaches a town, a new region or a named place, at daybreak, and sometimes when a hero calls out in the wild.
- The DM is told to end each turn with something the company can hear, and it stays around later into the day.
- When the DM names a place that is not on the map it is pointed to real names, or can mark the place first, and it can then use that place everywhere. Named creatures like Greymane can be talked about before they appear.
- The DM costs a little more: about $0.03 to $0.04 a game day, up from about $0.02, by estimate. A new DM on stage section in the behavior report shows what it said each day.
