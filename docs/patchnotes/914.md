- The buildings of every town now match the look of the heroes: pale stone and blue slate in Concord towns, dark timber and rust roofs in Warband towns.
- You can tell the places apart at a glance: a coin sign on the auction house, a sword over the open forge, a porch on the hall, a spire on the chapel and an INN sign on the inn.
- The buildings along the north wall now stand taller, with doors the right size for the heroes in front of them.
- Chimney smoke and the hall's flag rise from the new roofs.
