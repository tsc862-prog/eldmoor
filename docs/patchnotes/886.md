title: Fens, swamps, ash, snow, ice and lava now share the hand-drawn look
date: 2026-11-04
area: render
issues: [835]

- The fen, swamp, mud, ash rock, snow, ice and lava ground is now drawn in the same soft hand-drawn style as the grass and heroes.
- Where two kinds of ground meet, the edge is drawn too: snow with a frosty fringe over ice, grass tufts over mud, boulders over snow, a crust round the lava.
- Roads running through the fens, ash lands and snow have proper edges instead of a rough pixel blend.
- Trees and mountains now stand on the same ground as everything around them.
- Mountains, trees and rocks themselves are still the old look; they are next.
