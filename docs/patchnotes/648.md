---
title: DM triggers and watches
date: 2026-11-03
area: brain
issues: [635]
---
- The DM can set up a prepared action that fires later when something happens, such as a company entering a region, making camp, a hero levelling or a foe dying. It then narrates, places a person, stages a scene and so on.
- The DM can also set a watch that wakes it for a closer look when the same kind of thing happens. Fired actions follow the same daily limits and refusals as any other DM action and show in the DM log.
