---
title: Long games cost far less to run
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [787]
---
- Heroes now start a fresh conversation before it gets long enough to cost more. A turn should cost about a fifth of what it did late in a long run.
- A hero who starts fresh keeps its journal, notes and persona, and reads its journal first. It may forget what was just said, and this can happen every few turns.
- This never happens in the middle of a turn or while a scene waits for an answer.
- The runner's status line now shows how long the conversation is, for example ctx 83k. Past 100k means the expensive rate.
- Fresh session on the Start runner picker starts a hero with a new conversation, and Fresh sessions for all in the Agents panel does it for everyone at the next safe moment.
- A runner no longer stops dead when Windows has its save file open for a moment.
