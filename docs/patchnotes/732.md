---
title: Heroes who come to town to ask now stay to ask
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [727]
---
- A hero who walks into town on its own order now wakes at once and is not walked straight back out by autopilot for two game hours.
- A hero whose aim is to ask around, buy, sell or rest in town now waits there for it, and is told who is about and how to talk to them.
- A hero that asks to go to the town it already stands in is told it is already there, instead of walking out and back.
- An errand is short: it holds a hero in town four game hours at most, and a hero who leaves it undone is asked after two what it is really doing.
- Errands cannot be used to sit in town: a hero who keeps setting them and earns nothing is still sent out to hunt after twelve game hours.
