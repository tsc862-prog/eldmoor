---
title: A hero who drops at the table is down, not dead
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [654]
---
- A hero who drops to zero in a Table fight or a Crypt room is now down, not dead. They roll a death save each round, and companions can heal them, give them a potion or stabilize them as their turn.
- When a hero goes down out in the world with an agent nearby, a rescue scene opens and the world waits, so the agents get rounds to act instead of a timer.
- If nobody is left standing in a Crypt room, the downed die with the party. A party that is driven back or falls back often cannot carry a companion who is still bleeding, and they die below, so the Crypt stays deadly.
