---
title: Heroes stop posing the same way over and over
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [801]
---
- A hero who tries the same gesture again, or nearly the same, is told they already did that and should do something else, speak or wait.
- A gesture made with no one near to see it no longer counts as doing something, so a hero stuck on it is noticed as idle.
