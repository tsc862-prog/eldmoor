---
title: Fights at the table now play until they are decided
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [875]
---
- A fight at the table now plays until it is decided. The table no longer closes after three rounds and hands your heroes to autopilot mid-fight.
- A fight ends when the foe falls, runs or yields, when the whole party is down or has fallen back, or when the party withdraws.
- Very long fights are stopped as a safeguard: after twelve rounds (sixteen against Kragmaw), or when three rounds pass with nobody hurt. The Chronicle says the fight grinds on, and it carries on out in the world.
- The banner and the turn header now say "round 4" instead of "round 4 of 3".
