---
title: Where you stand now matters in the crypt
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [745]
---
- In a crypt room, melee heroes must stand next to a foe to hit it, and archers and casters need a clear line; a friend in the way spoils the shot.
- Two heroes on opposite sides of a foe hit it more easily, and a guard takes the blows beside it and shields whoever stands close.
- Stepping away from a foe gives it a free blow unless a guard covers you, and the party only leaves once most of them reach the stair.
- A Mage now blasts a square of the room and can catch friends in it, and a ward only reaches those near the one who speaks it.
- Foes walk too: swarms spread round you, archers keep their distance and a boss holds its ground until it is surrounded.
- Tending a fallen companion means getting to their side first, and a party driven out of a room now leaves its bleeding behind a little more often.
