---
title: Green heroes stop walking into the snow
date: 2026-11-05
area: ai
issues: [934]
---
- A hunt now stays in the region you sent it to, and takes only that region's own creatures with a clear way to them. Heroes no longer spot something across the river and walk round by the bridge to fight it.
- A hero who finds it is facing certain death breaks off at once, and from anything else beyond it at half health, instead of standing until it is nearly dead. Its agent is woken with what it faces and told it can flee.
- An agent can still pick that fight on purpose: an order to fight, naming the creature as prey or setting its own flee mark all stand. Death is as real as ever.
- At the start of a first delve the whole company now stands on the same bank of the river.
