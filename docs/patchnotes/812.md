---
title: Heroes swear their own oaths
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [800]
---
- Your company's heroes no longer start with an oath the game swore for them. Each is given a past, and a story from it is only offered.
- At the first table each hero may swear an oath in its own words, take up what its past holds, set it aside, or say nothing. The first evening asks once more, and then it never nags again.
- The narrator, the fireside, the Chronicle and the timeline speak only of oaths a hero really swore aloud.
- An oath naming a foe, a treasure, a place or a companion is followed by the game. Any other words stand until the hero says they are kept.
- A hero can now give up its sworn oath, at the same cost as breaking a vow. You can pin an oath in the sheet editor so it cannot be given up.
- Background heroes keep their oaths as before.
