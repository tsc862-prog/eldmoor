---
title: Art is made on your own free GPU Space
date: 2026-11-03
area: infra
issues: [703]
---
- New art is now drawn on your private Hugging Face GPU Space, using the 40 free GPU minutes a day that come with PRO, so it no longer touches the monthly credit, and the generator stops a few minutes before that daily allowance runs out.
- When the daily GPU time is used up, the art loop waits until exactly the moment it resets, not until the next day; press s in the launcher to see the images made and GPU minutes used today.
- One-time setup: make a token that can write to the Space, put it in your settings file, then run the Space push command once (the pull request lists the exact steps). The old paid route only runs when you ask for it by name.
