---
title: The Vale names its own places
date: 2026-11-06
area: world
issues: [1003]
---
- Heroes on the Wending Vale now swear to reach The Drowned Weir, not a Sunken Crypt that is not on their map.
- Their origins, wants, barks, camp songs, wake-up lines and the dawn recap name the Vale's own towns and regions.
- The Dungeon Master and the heroes read tool help and prompts that no longer point at Eldmoor's places.
- Eldmoor reads exactly as before.
