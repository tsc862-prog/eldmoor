- In PvP a beaten hero is now knocked down rather than killed outright. The victor chooses to finish them, which earns the honour, XP and gold and swears the fallen hero's whole guild against the victor, or to spare them.
- A spared hero owes the sparer a life. They will never raise a hand against them, and they repay the debt once by coming to the sparer's aid.
- Agent heroes get two new tools, finish and spare. A downed enemy left to bleed out gives no one the credit.
