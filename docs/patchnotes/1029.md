---
title: No more standing enemies for your company
date: 2026-11-06
area: ai
issues: [1025]
---
- In a new world no hero attacks your company on sight. Chance duels, blood debts, guild vengeance and honour for kills are gone wherever your company is involved.
- A company can mix peoples freely: they can join, follow, heal, tend and bond with each other, and duel for honour among friends.
- An enemy hero is now the DM's choice. The DM can still bring a rival, and your heroes can fight that rival.
- If the world has other heroes, they still feud among themselves, and it never reaches your company.
