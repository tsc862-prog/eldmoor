title: Roads between lands
date: 2026-11-06
area: ai
issues: [1013]
---
- Nothing you will notice yet: no map in the game has a road to another, and the screen cannot follow a company across until the next step.
- Behind the scenes a company can now leave one land for another. Its leader has to order it, and everyone decides together at the table: go, wait or turn back.
- The whole company goes or nobody does. If someone is hurt, fighting or out of earshot, the road waits and the leader is told why.
- The Dungeon Master frames the leaving and the first view of the new land, so a new map never opens in silence.
