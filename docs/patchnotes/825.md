title: A hero stuck repeating one gesture is noticed
date: 2026-11-04
area: brain
issues: [801]
---
- A hero played by a local model that keeps doing the very same gesture or line every turn is now treated as stuck, like one that does nothing, and its runner stops after a few turns.
- Waiting, hunting, walking and fighting the same way again are never counted as stuck.
- The behavior report names these loops.
