- Heroes now walk the map as detailed characters, one look for each class and each people, in the blue of the Concord or the red of the Warband.
- The Ashfolk are green-skinned and tusked, the Mirekin marsh-green, the Thornhide brown, and the three Concord peoples keep human skin tones with their own hair.
- Heroes of the same class and people still look alike for now; creatures, townsfolk and buildings keep the old art.
