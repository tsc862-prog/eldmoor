---
title: Heroes can say why they go the long way round
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [797]
---
- A move order now tells an agent hero in plain words what stands in the way and how it goes round: the river and which bridge, the town wall and the gate, cliffs or thick forest.
- When a walk is much longer than the straight line, the hero is told so, and a spot nobody can reach is said to be out of reach.
- Looking around now names what bars the land in sight and the nearest crossing or gate, and which known places have something in the way.
- Heroes have a new survey: the lay of the land around them, or the way to a place before they set out.
- Heroes only speak of what they can see or know: land they have never heard of stays unnamed, and the walk just bends.
- The DM's survey reads the same ground and can now give the way between two points.
