---
title: Fights on the map read more clearly
date: 2026-11-05
area: render
issues: [746]
---
- On a big patch like Kragmaw's lair, only a few tiles carry a cover shield now instead of nearly every tile; snow and swamp show a few slow marks instead of one on every tile.
- Words that float up over a hero, like "out of reach", are no longer hidden under the small tick or arrow above its head.
- A speech bubble follows its speaker while they walk to a new tile instead of jumping ahead.
- A foe that falls now visibly tips over and lies for a moment before it fades, and a fumble shows a real stumble, including for creatures facing left.
