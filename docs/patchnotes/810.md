---
title: Walls, rivers and peaks are solid ground
date: 2026-11-04
area: world
issues: [792, 791]
---
- The world now knows which ground cannot be stood on: town walls, river water, mountain peaks, lava, the Crypt's mouth rock, the wyrm's nest and the thick middles of the deepest forests.
- The Dungeon Master can no longer place a person, a marked place or a scene on that ground; the refusal names the nearest open spot instead.
- Camps are never pitched in the thick of a forest, so a few night camps may sit a few tiles from where they used to.
- Nothing walks differently yet: heroes still cross the map as before.
