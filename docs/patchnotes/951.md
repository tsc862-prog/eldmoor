---
title: The Wending Vale looks like a country
date: 2026-11-05
area: render
issues: [940]
---
- The river Wend now runs as one curving water from its head to the sea, with cut banks, a pale shore, lily pads in the slow reaches, reeds in the fen and ice near its source.
- Roads out in the country are proper tracks: a road on the diagonal no longer looks like a stair of squares.
- Each region has its own ground: Wendmouth has flower meadows and fields of wheat and cabbage round the towns, Hawkwold is yellower heath, Greywood is cooler and darker, and Ashenfold is scorched rock with cooled flows, ember cracks and bones.
- The crossings are places: a stone Low Bridge, a timber footbridge and a burnt High Bridge, and the Weirgate ford with stepping stones over bright shallows.
- The four dungeon mouths are cut into their own hills: the Drowned Weir with pools and braziers in a felled clearing, the Kestrel Barrow with standing stones on a green mound, the Sallow Vaults with barrels and crates, the Brandwright Kiln with ruined brick and fire.
- Kragmaw's nest has melted the snow to soot around it, with bones on the ground and skulls on stakes along the way in.
