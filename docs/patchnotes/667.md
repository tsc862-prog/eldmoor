---
title: Rumours in town and names for deeds
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [637, 638]
---
The DM can now leave a rumour with a town's innkeeper, gate captain and bard, who pass it on in their own words to heroes who talk to them or read the gate board, for up to five days.
A hero who hears a rumour about a place, a creature or a person learns of it, and remembers who told them.
After a great deed the DM can give a hero a name, such as "Wightbane" or "of the Ford", proclaimed by someone nearby or by the narrator.
That name then shows wherever the hero appears: their card, the party frames, the chronicle, the timeline and the Table banner.
A hero can refuse a name aloud, and the DM then gives them no other.
