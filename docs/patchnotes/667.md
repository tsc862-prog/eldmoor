---
title: Rumours and earned names
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [637, 638]
---
- The DM can leave a rumour in a town or region; for a few days the innkeeper, gate captain and bard pass it on in their own words when heroes talk to them or read the gate's jobs.
- Heroes hear each rumour once a day, and a place or foe it names becomes one they have heard of and can travel to.
- The DM can bestow a title on a hero, such as Wightbane, proclaimed by a townsperson nearby; it shows beside the hero's name on the map, the card, the frames and in the chronicle.
- A hero may refuse the name aloud, and then it is dropped for good.
