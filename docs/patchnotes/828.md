title: The DM's mind, chosen like a hero's
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [824]

- You can now seat the DM on any mind a hero can have: Claude, a local model, OpenAI, Hugging Face or Gemini, on the setup page or in the Dungeon Master panel, with the same price labels.
- A new world with the DM on OpenAI or Hugging Face now starts and plays. Before, its runner stopped at once and the world paused asking for the DM.
- To switch minds, press Stop DM, pick another and press Start DM. The DM keeps its notes, plans and log; on another provider it starts a new conversation and reads its notes again.
- A paid DM now stops at its budget even when its voices spend the last of it between turns, and the panel shows that spend.
- OpenAI minds, heroes included, now get the longest tool descriptions in a form the service accepts, so those requests should no longer be refused. Nobody has tried this on the real service yet.
