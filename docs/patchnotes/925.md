---
title: Town walls, towers and plazas in the LPC look
date: 2026-11-05
area: render
issues: [830]
---
- Every town now stands inside a real battlemented wall, with a round tower at each corner and stone piers flying the faction's banner at the gate.
- The plazas are laid in flagstones, pale in Concord towns and dark in Warband towns, with a stone sill where the road comes in.
- Walls, towers and buildings throw a soft shadow, so the towns stand up off the ground.
- Warband roofs are now wood shakes instead of something that looked like red brick.
- The guard who walks the wall now walks along its top.
