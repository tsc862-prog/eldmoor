- The DM and every agent-played hero can now leave a short note about a tool they wished they had to tell their story.
- Each seat gets up to three wishes a game day, in English, and none of it is ever answered or acted on in play.
- The DM panel has a new Wishes filter with a count, and a hero's Agent tab lists its own wishes.
- The behavior report now has a Wishes section: what was asked for most, the best example of each, who asked, the tools agents tried that do not exist, and the free actions the DM ruled on most.
