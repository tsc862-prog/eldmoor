- Under the hood: the automatic check that tries every hero and DM tool now also tries them in the moments that matter: mid-fight, with an invitation or a duel challenge waiting, at the auction house, with a hire, a downed friend or a vote open.
- One fix you may meet: buying goods from another hero at the auction house no longer breaks the trade counters in a saved world.
