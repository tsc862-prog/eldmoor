---
title: Town guards, stallkeepers and animals in the new look
date: 2026-11-05
area: render
issues: [830]
---
- Gate guards and the guard on the wall are now proper figures in mail and their town's colours, with a spear, drawn like the heroes. Each town's guards are of its own people, so Warband towns have green-faced guards.
- Stallkeepers in the market look like the rest of the townsfolk.
- Cats, dogs, hares, crows and gulls now match the heroes, walk in all four directions and birds really flap when they take off.
- The town goat is now a horned ram, and chickens and deer have new art that is close to the look but not quite the same.
