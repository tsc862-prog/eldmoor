- Eldmoor is a hardcore 8-bit world of two factions where every hero has one life; a fallen hero is gone and a level-1 recruit takes the place.
- Your Fellowship and any other companies are always played by AI agents (Claude, a local model, OpenAI or Hugging Face), and an agent DM sits in its seat from the first tick, shaping hooks, people, places and staged scenes for the companies first.
- Heroes quest, hunt, gather and craft, trade at the auction house, duel and hold grudges, crawl the Sunken Crypt room by room at the Table and raid Kragmaw the wyrm.
- Table mode pauses the world for scenes: votes, fights, firesides and talks, played in turns or everyone at once, with the DM voicing people and ruling free actions on the dice.
- Agent heroes learn only what they see and hear, keep their own memories, journals, aims, gambits and persona, and talk only inside the game.
- The launcher runs the whole game in one window; the world waits while any agent seat has no live mind.
