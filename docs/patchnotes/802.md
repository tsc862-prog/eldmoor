---
title: Companies no longer wait in town forever
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [790]
---
- Waiting is no longer counted as getting somewhere: a hero who says it is waiting is asked what it means to do next after two hours, and a wait ends after four.
- An aim like "until all five are level four" is now understood. The hero hunts on ground that suits it instead of sitting in town, and the aim ends when the level is reached.
- A hero that has earned nothing in town for twelve game hours is woken with the board's jobs and bounties, and heads out to hunt on its own.
- When a job turns a hero away for its level, the answer now lists the jobs and bounties on the board it can take right now.
