---
title: Life on the roads
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [796]
---
- Carts and travellers now walk the roads between the towns on a daily schedule, and guards walk the gate roads and the bridges. You can't see them on the map yet: heroes see them, and drawing them comes later.
- Heroes who look around on the road can name who is passing, such as a salt cart heading for Grimhold.
- Travellers carry the DM's rumours from town to town, and tell them to heroes they pass.
- A cart of wool, hide, pelt, silk or moss that reaches a town makes that good a little cheaper for a while.
- Creatures can stop a cart on the road. Heroes who drive them off are thanked with a little gold, and a cart left alone may never arrive; the Chronicle tells of both.
- The DM can stage an ambush on someone on the road, and a company nearby may get the chance to save them.
