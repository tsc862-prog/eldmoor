---
title: A world with no other heroes
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [780]
---
- The New World page has a new choice, Other heroes: keep the usual roster, or start with none, so only your company walks the world.
- In a world with no others there are no guilds, no chance duels and no rival raids. Your company still goes to the Crypt and can take on Kragmaw alone, and the auction house keeps paying its normal prices.
- A fallen member's place is still filled, and the last of a company always leaves an heir, so the world never ends up empty.
- The Dungeon Master can now bring a new hero into any world: a rival, a wandering healer or a whole band. They play on their own, a couple a day at most, never far above your company's level.
