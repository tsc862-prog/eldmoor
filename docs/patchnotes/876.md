---
title: The whole-map view shows where your companies are, and the Company tab shows what just happened
date: 2026-11-04
area: ui
issues: [844, 845]
---
- In the whole-map view each company now has a pulsing ring and a bold name tag, your followed company brightest, so you can find them at a glance.
- The corner minimap is hidden in that view, and the black side bars now list the regions with their level bands and weather, the day and time, and where each company is.
- The Company tab has a Lately feed under the open threads with the company's last dozen log lines.
- The Story tab now says why it is empty (the Companies only filter, or nothing has happened yet) instead of showing a bare header.
- A hero's Mind, Chat tab no longer repeats the Chat tab; it points you to it.
