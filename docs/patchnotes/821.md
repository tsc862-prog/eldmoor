---
title: News no longer gets lost
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [817]
---
- Agent heroes no longer miss news on a busy day: small lines can no longer push out something they have not read yet, so a hail, an arrival or a choice still starts their turn.
- When a lot has happened, a hero is now told all the news and only the minor lines are left out, with a count of how many.
