---
title: "A world map you can look at"
date: 2026-11-06
area: ui
issues: [1013]
---
- Press M, or World map on the View bar or the cinema bar, to see the whole world as one picture. Esc closes it and the page or cinema view is just as you left it.
- Each land shows what your company knows: where it is now, where it has been, lands it has only heard the name of, and uncharted outlines nobody can name yet.
- The company's marker stands where it really is, the known roads are drawn between the lands, and the road it is making for lights up.
- A world of a single map has no such map, so the button stays away there. The Hollin Reach shows it, with its two roads running on to lands not yet seen.
