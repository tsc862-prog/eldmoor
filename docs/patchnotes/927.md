---
title: A fresh Fellowship replacement is left alone for a while
date: 2026-11-05
area: ai
issues: [660]
---
- When a Fellowship member falls and a new one is sent for, enemy heroes no longer pick a duel with the newcomer for their first eight game hours.
- This stops the rare run where one rival band kept cutting down each replacement in turn.
- Old grudges still count, and nothing else about death changes: it stays permanent.
