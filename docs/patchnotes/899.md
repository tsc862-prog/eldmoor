---
title: Mountains are real mountains now
date: 2026-11-04
area: render
issues: [895]
---
- Mountains are no longer flat grey slabs: they are ragged cliffs with rock faces and tops, stepping up in tiers, with peaks standing on them.
- Each land has its own rock: brown stone in the highlands, dark rock in the ash lands, and snow-covered cliffs and snowy peaks in the far north.
- Lava, ice and roads now meet a mountain cleanly, because the cliffs stand on the ground around them.
- Rough borders are finer: grass and mud now fringe the ash rock, swamp banks meet the snow, and ice beside the fens, swamp beside meadows and mud beside rivers have proper edges.
