- In the dungeon view the room being fought fills the screen, and each hero walks to where their choice puts them: the guard to the front, strikers beside the foes, healers at the back, scouts into cover, the retreating back to the stair.
- Foes stand at hero size with a red outline, a name and a health bar, and creatures in water show ripples so they are easy to find.
- Each round plays out: strikes lunge, hits flash and shake, a roll of 1 makes the hero stumble, and the damage floats up before the round's summary appears under the room.
