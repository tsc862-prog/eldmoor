---
title: A hero's turn at the table no longer waits behind companions
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [868]
---
- A hero whose turn comes at the table no longer waits behind companions who are only waiting for them, so the world stops standing still for minutes and heroes stop losing their turn to a reflex.
- Once a hero has answered at the table their turn simply ends, and they are woken when the table settles or asks them again.
- One Claude place is now kept free for answers at the table; with the default of three, two free turns run at once and the third place waits for a scene.
- Places that nobody was really using now come back by themselves, so the runner bar no longer counts a place that is not in use.
