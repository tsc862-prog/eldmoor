title: "The chapter towns and lands each look like themselves"
date: 2026-11-07
area: render
issues: [1013]
---
- Oldstead in the Hollin Reach has straw-gold roofs, honey-coloured walls and a draw-well on its green instead of a fountain.
- Gullstrand on the Wendmouth is a whitewashed harbour town under sea-green slate, with red-and-white market stalls.
- Hawkstead in Hawkwold is red clay tiles over grey stone and dark granite walls.
- The Sallowfen is dark peat instead of rust-red mud, its edges fray into the grass, and reeds and lily pads grow along its pools.
- Woods such as Withywood mix in birches, copper beeches and dead trees, with bushes and fallen logs among them.
