---
title: Dungeon view shows what a chamber held and what was taken
date: 2026-11-06
area: ui
issues: [976]
---
- Click a chamber in the dungeon view and each foe now shows the roll needed to strike it.
- The same detail tells you what the last clear paid, or what a clear would pay if nobody has managed one yet.
- History now says what each run took: the gold, the goods and who got any treasure. A wipe or a retreat reads "Nothing taken".
- After a clear, every chamber shows an open chest beside its tick. The chests go as soon as the next group goes down.
- Clears from older saves read "not recorded".
