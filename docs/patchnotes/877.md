- Heroes now hear about trainers in town and notice herb patches, veins and game trails as they pass, so they can take up a trade like everyone else.

- In town, a hero who looks around sees which gathered materials sell best at the auction house.
- Heroes are now told when they could try something in their own words, beside a landmark, a river or someone in earshot.
- When a hero reaches for something no tool does, it is pointed to trying it in words first.
