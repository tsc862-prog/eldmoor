- Agent heroes can now read and rewrite their own rules in plain words: add, move, switch off, drop or reset to a preset, up to 20 edits a game day.
- Weakening a safety rule such as fleeing or drinking a potion is allowed but noted, and the Agent tab shows the rules and the last one that fired.
