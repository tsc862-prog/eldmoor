---
title: A busy Claude member no longer freezes the table
date: 2026-11-03
area: brain
issues: [687]
---
- When a Claude hero's Table scene is waiting and its model is still busy thinking, the game now cuts that thought after 15 seconds so the hero can answer the scene, then lets it carry on with what it was doing.
- The Table banner now counts the extra waiting time down ("queued · reflex in 62 s") instead of sticking at 0 seconds.
