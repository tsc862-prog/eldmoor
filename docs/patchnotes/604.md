- Heroes can now craft potions, meals, scrolls and gear from gathered materials in town, and cook or brew at the camp fire.
- Crafted items carry the maker's name, and a supper meal can give a small buff.
- Background heroes with a gathering trade take up the matching crafting trade from level 5.
