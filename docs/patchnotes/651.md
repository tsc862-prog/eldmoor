---
title: Visions at the table
date: 2026-11-03
area: brain
issues: [639]
---
- The DM can stage a vision, an omen or shared dream, that opens when a company next rests or stands at a chosen place. There are no dice and no harm.
- Each member present says what they see, asks one question or turns away. Those who looked learn the place or creature it showed; those who turned away learn nothing.
