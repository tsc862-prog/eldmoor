---
title: "A new map: the Hollin Reach"
date: 2026-11-06
area: world
issues: [1013]
---
- A new map to choose on the title screen: the Hollin Reach, one small walled town with fields, a wood and a river, for levels 1 to 5. Eldmoor is still the default.
- It holds less and leaves more room: long walks from the gate, and creatures in small groups far apart.
- Your company opens with session zero and the first delve as usual, at the Tithe Grange at the end of the wood road.
- Up on the fell, the Quarry Cellars give a level 3 to 5 company a real dungeon to go to.
- Two roads run off the map, north and east. They are not open yet: order a walk along one and your heroes are told so.
- No other side walks this map, and any company may rest in the town.
