---
title: "Heroes hear of other lands, and can set out for them"
date: 2026-11-06
area: brain
issues: [1013]
---
- Heroes now learn of lands past the map's edge the way they learn anything: from the gate, from townsfolk, from each other, from a rumour the DM lets loose.
- A leader can name a place in another land and the company walks to the right road, even when the way runs through a land between; the road out is still a decision for the whole company at the table.
- The DM knows which land is live, sees what lies along each road, and can leave something waiting for the company's arrival in a new land.
- A bounty, a meeting or a marked spot on the land you left stays there, and is waiting if you come back. Worlds with one map play exactly as before.
