---
title: Every town takes your company in
date: 2026-11-06
area: ai
issues: [1025]
---
- In a new world no town turns your heroes away: they can walk into any town and nobody there calls it enemy ground.
- Trainers, the smith, the workshops and the chapel priest serve your heroes in any town, and the townsfolk answer them.
- Your heroes can walk straight to a named spot in another town, such as its chapel, instead of stopping at the gate.
- The DM can stage scenes and bring people in at any town.
- Job boards and couriers still work from your company's own hall.
