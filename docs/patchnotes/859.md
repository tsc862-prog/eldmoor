---
title: Nothing walks through walls
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [794]
---
- Heroes, monsters and townsfolk now walk around walls, houses, peaks, lava and rivers instead of straight through them.
- Rivers are real: everyone crosses at a bridge, or wades at the ford in the south of the Stepping Lands. The ford has no art of its own yet.
- Heroes follow the roads when the road is quicker, so most journeys take a little less time than before.
- Nobody picks a fight across a river or through a wall any more, and a monster that cannot reach its prey gives up and goes home.
- A move order now tells an agent hero how long the way really is, what stands in the straight line, and which bridge or ford it will use. It only names places the hero already knows.
- Raiders still reach Kragmaw: only the rock at the very heart of the lair is solid.
