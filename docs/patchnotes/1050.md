---
title: "A company stays together on the way to town"
date: 2026-11-06
area: ai
issues: [1048]
---
- A company no longer falls apart when it walks into a town, on any map, so the leader can lead it straight out again.
- If a company does lose its party, after a death or a dungeon, it now forms up again where it stands instead of waiting for a town.
- Any company's leader can now use lead_to, not only the Fellowship's; for a hero alone it is a plain walk.
- When one member runs for home, the rest no longer turn back with them. Companions with no order of their own come to the town the leader is in.
- The road bar stops saying On the way once the leader has camped or gone off hunting instead of walking to the road.
