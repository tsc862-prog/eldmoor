- When a company settles at camp with an agent at the table, a short fireside scene can open: members talk about the day, ask each other questions and tell stories from their past.
- Nights after a death, a boss or a newcomer run longer. Stories told are remembered by the listeners.
- A short debrief follows a rescue, a fall or a close vote. Townsfolk now remember each hero they talk to.
