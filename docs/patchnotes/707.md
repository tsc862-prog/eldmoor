---
title: Honour duels
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [633]
---
- Heroes can now challenge a hero of their own faction to an honour duel, to first blood or until one yields, and fight it out at the table in turns.
- No one dies or goes down in an honour duel. The winner gains honour, both remember it, and a leader beaten by a companion hands over the lead.
