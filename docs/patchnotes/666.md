---
title: Hire a sellsword or guide
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [634]
---
- Heroes can hire a sellsword, drover or guide for a few gold a day, paid up front for one to five days; the hireling rides with the company but never leads or votes.
- A hired guide warns of danger ahead and tells the company about the region it knows.
- When the days run out or the hero dismisses them, hirelings go home and remember the company; a hireling who falls is mourned, and their town is wary of hiring out for a few days.
- The DM can have a townsperson offer their service for hire.
