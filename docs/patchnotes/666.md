---
title: Heroes can hire a sellsword or guide
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [634]
---

- A hero can now hire a sellsword, drover or guide standing nearby for a few gold a day (1 to 5 days, paid up front).
- The hire rides with the company as a fighter who follows the leader, never leads and never votes.
- A guide knows the roads: its warning joins the danger warning and its region shows up as heard of.
- When the days are up, or the hero pays them off, they walk home. If one falls, it stays dead and the town mourns.
- The DM can have a person offer their blade at a price during a conversation.
