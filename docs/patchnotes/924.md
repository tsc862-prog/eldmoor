---
title: A cheaper way to carry the heroes' tools, off until you turn it on
date: 2026-11-05
area: brain
issues: [808]
---
- Nothing changes unless you choose it: add the line AGENT_LAZY_TOOLS=1 to your .env file and restart the runners to try it.
- With it on, Claude heroes keep their common tools, their oaths, journal, memories and everything a scene needs at hand, and look up the rarer ones when they want them. Every tool is still there.
- Each hero's model call should carry about a sixth less fixed text, which should lower the cost of a long run. This has only been tried against a stand-in, not with a paid model yet.
- Turning it on or off starts each hero on a fresh session at its next safe moment, as a roll does.
- The behavior report gains a Tool loading table once you try it, so you can see the difference for yourself. The Dungeon Master and local models are not affected.
