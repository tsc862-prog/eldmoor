---
title: A spent budget now waits for you
date: 2026-11-04
area: brain
issues: [807]
---
- When a runner has spent its budget, the bar over the map now says how much ("budget spent: $2.05 of $2.00"), and each button says what it will spend, such as "Restart · another $2.00".
- Nothing starts a spent runner again without you: not a relay restart, not a hero leaving a company, not an heir taking a seat. The world stays paused until you choose.
- Restart now takes one click. Before, it stopped the runner and you had to press a second button to start it.
- If no session budget is set, the bar, the Agents panel and the runner pickers say so, so you can see that nothing caps the total. None is set for you.
- A crash, an heir after any other kind of stop, and a hero leaving a company with money left work as before.
