- Press C to make the map the whole window, and C or Esc to come back. The Cinema button in the View group does the same, and the title screen has a switch to open your world in it.
- A director camera follows your company: it pulls out on the road, closes in for a fight or a Table scene, and drifts slowly when nothing is happening.
- Now and then it cuts away to something worth seeing, such as a duel, a raid on the wyrm or a cart beset on the road, and comes back. Cuts dip to black, and a line says what you are looking at.
- All that stays on screen is a slim plate for each hero, the clock, the last line or two of the story, and the Table banner when a scene is open.
- Only your company and the hero under the pointer are named. Move the pointer to get speed buttons and Leave; click a plate or press 1-9 to look at that hero.
- If the world is waiting for minds or a runner stops, the banner still shows over the map and its buttons work there.
