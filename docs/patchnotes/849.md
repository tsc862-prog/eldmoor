title: Towns and country come alive
date: 2026-11-04
area: render
issues: [795]
---
- Towns now keep chickens, dogs, cats and goats in the yard. Deer, hares, crows and gulls live in the country by region, and they scatter when a hero comes within two tiles and wander home afterwards.
- Birds roost in a few trees and burst out when a hero walks past.
- Market stalls open in the plaza in market hours with a keeper behind the table, and shut under a tarp at night and in the rain.
- Guards flank each gate and one walks the wall opposite. The smithy smokes through the working day and the inn from breakfast and from the afternoon, drifting with the wind.
- At dusk the doors get lamps and the forge glows; a pennant in the town's colours flies over the hall.
- Heroes leave footprints in snow that fill in again. The smithy, chapel, inn and notice board now show on the map as simple buildings.
