---
title: Heroes try more in their own words, and the DM's ruling reaches them more often
date: 2026-11-05
area: ai
issues: [864]
---
- After the narrator describes something, a hero is told it can try something about it in its own words.
- Heroes standing at a lair's edge or a crypt's mouth are now told they could try something there.
- The DM's own ruling on what a hero tries now arrives more often instead of a stock answer, also when the world runs at high speed.
- When the DM's first ruling breaks the rules of what it may do, it gets one more try.
