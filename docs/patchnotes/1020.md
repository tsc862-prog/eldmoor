---
title: A first job a green company can take
date: 2026-11-06
area: ai
issues: [1016]
---
- Session zero no longer sends a level 1 company to a hall job that needs level 4; it says the job is beyond them and names where to hunt first.
- Putting a job to the party's vote that the company cannot take is now turned back at once, with the reason, instead of passing and being refused at the gate.
- In a live game the hall board no longer posts a job the new company would be turned away from, and the DM is warned when a job it posts asks for more than the company has.
