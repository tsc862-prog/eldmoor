---
title: The Wending Vale gets its own creatures
date: 2026-11-05
area: creatures
issues: [940]
---
- Each region of the Wending Vale now has its own beasts, from Strand Wolves at the river mouth to Crown Wights on the snowy crown, each with its own trade good and bounty.
- The fen and the highland keep gentler beasts near their home towns, so new heroes are not met by creatures well above them.
- A new rare elite, Grimbrand the Kiln-Golem, haunts the Ashenfold.
- The boss chambers of the Vale's dungeons last more rounds, so a bold company has time to win.
