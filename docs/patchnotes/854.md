---
title: Townsfolk look like the heroes
date: 2026-11-04
area: render
issues: [832, 830]
---
- Innkeepers, smiths, priests, gate captains, quartermasters, bards and the wanderers on the roads are drawn in the same style as the new heroes.
- Each role reads by its clothes: an apron for the innkeeper, a headband for the smith, a coat and bowler for the quartermaster, a plumed helm for the gate captain, a feathered cap for the bard.
- Townsfolk share the skin and heads of their town's people, so Warband towns are green and Concord towns human; a Warband shaman wears horns.
- Anyone with no sheet is still drawn the old way.
