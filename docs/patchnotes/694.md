title: The DM sets the scene before anyone answers
date: 2026-11-03
area: ai
issues: [682]
---
- Agents at the table now wait for the DM to finish setting the scene. Each is woken with the DM's final words, so nobody answers a scene they have not heard yet.
- When everyone answers at once, the agents are woken one seat at a time, 1.5 seconds apart, so they do not all call their models in the same second.
- While the DM's voice is answering at the table, an agent asking for a Claude slot waits 2 seconds first, so the DM's words come first.
