---
title: A better recipe for generated town buildings
date: 2026-11-04
area: art
issues: [763]
---
- Generated buildings must now look like the game's own houses: seen from above with a big roof, a few flat colours, a thick dark outline and a dark footing strip. Grey, speckled pictures are turned away automatically.
- No building is taken in until it has been looked at in a real town on the full screen, next to the drawn houses.
