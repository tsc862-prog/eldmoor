- The Tithe Grange now stands at the end of the wood road as a broken stone ruin with a stair going down, instead of an empty clearing.
- Each road that runs off the Hollin Reach's edge ends at a last milestone, a worn stone with an arrow showing where the road goes on.
- The Quarry Cellars' mouth is dressed as a quarry: pale cut stone, blocks, cairns and a wedge crate.
