---
title: Kragmaw survives a reload
date: 2026-11-05
area: ai
issues: [324]
---
- Kragmaw no longer vanishes when you reload a world. Before, any reload left the wyrm gone for good and raids stopped; a world saved earlier gets a whole Kragmaw back at full health.
- The Chronicler's dawn recap no longer retells old deaths after a reload.
- A reloaded world now carries on exactly as it would have, down to townsfolk strolls and scripted lines.
