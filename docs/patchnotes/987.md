- Press C and the cinema view now opens close on your company, with heroes about 100 pixels tall on a 3440 wide screen, and it stays close on the road too.
- When the company sets out the camera pulls back for a few seconds to show the land, then eases in again. Fights and Table scenes go closer still.
- If the company scatters, the camera stays close on the leader and whoever is near them.
- The caption now sits on a soft dark shade, so the words read over roofs and behind the plates.
