---
title: The DM can ask a company to the Crypt or a raid
date: 2026-11-04
area: ai
issues: [826]
---
- The world still never asks, but your DM now can: a gate captain, someone the DM placed or the narrator puts the Crypt or a raid to a company in plain speech.
- A company answers with its usual vote, shown as that person's question, and the members decide. The DM only asks.
- A hero riding alone answers out loud, yes or no, and a yes sets them walking to the Crypt or riding with the muster. Saying nothing for a few game hours counts as no.
- The DM can ask a company once a game day, and a no keeps it from asking again for two game days.
- The DM won't ask while someone in the company is badly hurt out in the field, or while they are in a fight, a scene, the Crypt or a raid.
- The DM now knows when a group is gathering at the Crypt or a muster is out, and heroes only hear of it if the DM tells them.
