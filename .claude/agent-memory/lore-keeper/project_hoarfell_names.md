On 2026-10-31 the Hoarfell zone (levels 16+, Cinderpass narrowed to 11-15) and all its
names passed the originality check: Hoarfell, Rimehound, Tundra Shaghorn, Tarn Wight,
quests "Howls on the Snowfield" / "Wool for the Weavers" / "What the Tarn Keeps", goods
Rime Pelt / Hoar Wool / Wight Marrow, epics Rime-Etched Warpick / Shaghorn Greatcloak /
Icevein Diadem. Nearest echoes noted (none blocking): "Rime-" prefix appears on WoW frost
items (Rimefang, Rimeblade); "Tundra" evokes WoW's Borean Tundra zone; "wight" is a
generic undead term (Tolkien, D&D).

**Why:** the parent agent asked for a per-name pass/fail; recording the outcome avoids
re-litigating the same names and documents how close an echo is still acceptable.

**How to apply:** house patterns to keep for future zones:
- Quest titles: "<Thing> for the <Trade>" (Tusks for the Tanner, Wool for the Weavers),
  "<Event> on/in the <Place>" (Ash on the Wind, Howls on the Snowfield), or a short
  evocative phrase (What the Tarn Keeps).
- Epics: one "<Material>-<Verb>ed <Weapon>" (Ember-Forged, Rime-Etched), one zone-creature
  garment, one headpiece/crown per zone.
- Trade goods are plain two-word materials; price ladder climbs per zone (see
  creature-designer memory). Known-stale text outside my files as of 2026-10-31:
  `CLAUDE.md` Layout and `.claude/agents/world-builder.md` still say "Cinderpass 11+".
